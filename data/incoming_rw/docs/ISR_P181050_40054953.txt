1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 09-Sep-2024)
   The Program Development Objective is to improve employability for target beneficiaries and strengthen institutional
   capacity for enhanced service delivery in skills development.

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   Overall, there is good implementation progress of the Program. As reported in the recent semi-annual progress report of
   September 2025, implementation of all the Disbursement Linked Indicators (DLIs) is advancing satisfactorily. Particularly,
   implementation of the Skills Development Fund (SDF): 286 companies and institutions are training the first cohort of
   15,495 SDF trainees; Labor Market Information System (LMIS) user needs, and customer journey were defined and
   validated; and new National Employment and Skills Strategy (NESS) was developed and approved.
