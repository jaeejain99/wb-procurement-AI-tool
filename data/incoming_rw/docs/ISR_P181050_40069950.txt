3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   Program implementation is progressing well. Implementation of all the Disbursement Linked Indicators (DLIs) is
   advancing satisfactorily. Particularly, implementation of the Skills Development Fund (SDF): 282 companies and
   institutions are training the first cohort of 15,495 SDF trainees. The Labor Market Information System (LMIS) user needs,
   and customer journey were defined and validated, and an upgrade of the LMIS is ongoing based on the user feedback
   reports; new National Employment and Skills Strategy (NESS) was developed and approved and a total of 33,643 TVET
   and University students have received laptops. The Government has also approved key legal reforms to improve the
   performance of University of Rwanda under the relevant DLI.
