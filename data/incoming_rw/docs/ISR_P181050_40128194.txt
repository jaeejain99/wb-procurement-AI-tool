3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
Overall, there is good implementation progress of the Program. As reported in the semi-annual progress report of June
2026, implementation of all the Disbursement Linked Indicators (DLIs) is advancing satisfactorily. Under the Disbursement
Linked Indicator (DLI) Skills Development Fund (SDF), 17,352 trainees (of which, 9,504 females) have completed their training and a tracer study for this cohort is ongoing; the Rwanda TVET Board has issued service orders to training institutions with existing framework contracts to implement a second intake of SDF training targeting targeting an additional 20,000 trainees by early 2027. A Graduate Tracer Study for long-term training in target TVET and university programs (energy, transport and logistics, manufacturing, agro-processing) was completed. The Phase 1 upgrade of teaching and learning facilities for target training programs at University of Rwanda and Rwanda Polytechnic is nearing completion, with all target equipment installed, related staff trainings conducted and commissioning underway.
