4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project was approved by the World Bank Board on May 24, 2024, legal agreements were signed on May 31, 2024,
  and the project became effective on September 23, 2024. Since effectiveness, implementation has commenced with
  strong Government commitment and coordination with development partners. As of December 9, 2025, disbursement
  stands at approximately 7 percent. Management and the Government are prioritizing measures to accelerate
  implementation and disbursements while maintaining alignment with the project’s development objectives and the
  multiphase programmatic approach.
