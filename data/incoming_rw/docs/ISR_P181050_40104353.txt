3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
Program implementation is advanced and considered satisfactory. Program absorption is at 52 percent in the two years
of Program implementation. Disbursement Linked Indicators (DLIs) and associated Disbursement Linked Results (DLRs)
are initiated and advanced. Notably, the Skills Development Fund (SDF) is advanced. In all, 17,352 trainees (9,504
females) have completed their training from 282 competitively recruited industry companies/institutions as SDF Grantees
and authorized 69 private institutions authorized by RTB to provide self-paying short courses. A tracer study for this
cohort will be completed by October 2026. The cohort comprises youth not in education, or employment, youth with
special needs and disabilities, existing workers in small and medium and university/TVET graduates. A tracer study to
track transition to employment will be completed by October 2026. The Graduate Tracer Study for long term training in
target TVET and University programs of Energy, Transport and Logistics, Manufacturing (Agro-processing) is nearing
completion by May 31, 2026. The Phase 1 upgrade of teaching and learning facilities is advanced. A legal framework
establishing the Sector Skills Council (SSCs) was approved by stakeholders and the SSC for construction is fully operational
