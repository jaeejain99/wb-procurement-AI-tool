Procurement Plan for the Project. The following conditions apply to all procurement activities in the Procurement Plan. The other elements of the
Procurement Plan as required under paragraph 4.4 of the Procurement
Regulations are set forth in STEP.
The Bank’s Standard Procurement Documents: shall be used for all contracts subject to international competitive procurement and those contracts as specified in the Procurement Plan tables in STEP.
National Procurement Arrangements: In accordance with paragraph 5.3 of the Procurement Regulations, when approaching the national market (as specified in the Procurement Plan tables in STEP), the country’s own procurement procedures may be used.
When the Borrower uses its own national open competitive procurement arrangements as set forth in [Public Procurement & Asset Disposal
Regulations, 2015 and its Attendant Public Procurement & Asset
Disposal Regulations, 2020], such arrangements shall be subject to paragraph 5.4 of the Procurement Regulations and the following conditions.
• Procurement is open to eligible firms from any country
• Bidders/Proposers submitting Bids/Proposals to present a signed
acceptance at the time of bidding, to b e incorporated in any resulting contracts, confirming application of and compliance with the Bank’s Anti-Corruption Guidelines, including without limitation the Bank’s right to sanction and the Bank’s inspection and audit rights
• Contracts with an appropriate allocation of responsibilities, risks
and liabilities
• An effective complaint handling mechanism
• Inclusion of ES provisions acceptable to the Bank
When other national procurement arrangements other than national open competitive procurement arrangements are applied by the Borrower, such arrangements shall be subject to paragraph 5.5 of the Procurement
Regulations.
