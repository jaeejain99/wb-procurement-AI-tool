Preamble
In accordance with paragraph 5.9 of the “World Bank Procurement Regulations for IPF
Borrowers” (July 2016) (“Procurement Regulations”) the Bank’s Systematic Tracking and
Exchanges in Procurement (STEP) system will be used to prepare, clear and update
Procurement Plans and conduct all procurement transactions for the Project.
This textual part along with the Procurement Plan tables in STEP constitute the
Procurement Plan for the Project. The following conditions apply to all procurement activities in the Procurement Plan. The other elements of the Procurement Plan as required under paragraph 4.4 of the Procurement Regulations are set forth in STEP.
The Bank's Standard Procurement Files shall be used for all contracts subject to international competition, as well as for all other contracts subject to this obligation as specified in the tables of the STEP procurement plan.

National procurement procedures: In accordance with paragraph 5.3 of the
Procurement Rules, in the event that the procurement procedure is conducted at the national level (as specified in the tables of the procurement plan included in STEP), the national procurement procedures of the country concerned may be used.
 If the Borrower uses its own open national tender procedures as set out in the Public
Procurement Act, 2003 (Act 663), which was amended in 2016 by the Public
Procurement (Amendment) Act, 2016, (Act 914), and the related implementing texts, such arrangements shall be subject to the provisions of paragraph 5.4 of the
Procurement Rules and to the following conditions:
• The tender/proposal documents oblige the Bidder(s) submitting a Bid/Proposal to
submit a signed approval, which will be attached to any contract, to confirm that it complies with the Bank's Anti-Corruption Guidelines, including without limitation, the Bank's right to sanction and the Bank's rights for inspection and audit;
• The procurement documents contain arrangements agreed with the Bank to
mitigate environmental, social (including sexual exploitation and abuse and gender-based violence), and health and safety ("HSS") risks and impacts;
• The Bank's right to review the file and procurement activities.
 Where the Borrower uses procedures other than the national open tender, such procedures shall be in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 5.5 of the
Procurement Rules.
Leasing as specified in paragraph 5.10 of the Procurement Regulations: Leasing may be used for contracts indicated as such in the Procurement Plan. "Not applicable"
Purchase of second-hand goods as specified in paragraph 5.11 of the Procurement
Regulations is permitted for contracts indicated as such in the Procurement Plan. "Not applicable"
National preference as specified in paragraph 5.51 of the Procurement (Supplies and
Works) Regulations.
Hands-on Expanded Implementation Support (HEIS) as specified in paragraphs 3.10 and 3.11 of the Procurement Regulations does not apply.
