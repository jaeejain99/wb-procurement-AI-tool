1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 26-Apr-2023)
   To support the Government of Bangladesh to transition to green and climate resilient development by (i) enhancing
   public planning, financing, and delivery of green and climate resilient interventions; and (ii) promoting key sector reforms
   for greener and more efficient production and services.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

  Comments
  Overall, the program is being properly managed at each line agency and coordinated by the Ministry of Finance (MoF).
  However, there is room to improve the monitoring and evaluation (M&E) system in place. The Bank team is proposing a
  more structured M&E plan to the MoF, including regular meetings and consolidated progress reports based on inputs
  from line agencies.



3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The Government of Bangladesh (GoB) has reiterated its commitments towards green and climate resilient development
   (GCRD). As expected, it takes some time for the ministries to build awareness among their staff and citizens, and to fully
