3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The second operation of three operations in the Bangladesh Programmatic Green and Climate Resilient Development
  Policy Credit series was approved on December 19, 2024 and declared legally effective on December 23, 2024. It was
  fully disbursed on December 24, 2024. The programmatic series’ development objective is to support the Government of
  Bangladesh to transition to green and climate resilient development by (a) enhancing public planning, financing, and
  delivery of green and climate resilient interventions; and (b) promoting key sector reforms for clean and resource
  efficient production and services.

  Ten policy actions have been successfully developed and approved by the government and are now being
  implemented. They are: the incorporation of green, climate resilience (GCR) objectives and results indicators in Multi-
  Year Public Investment Programme Guidelines (MYPIPs) and Medium-Term Budget Frameworks (MBFs) of key sectoral
  agencies (PA1); the adoption of the DAF (PA2); the adoption of new guidelines for ULGIs to apply GCR criteria in local
  planning, financing, and implementation of their investments and services financed by discretionary sources (PA3); the
  establishment of the Designated National Authority with the mandate to approve, authorize and track carbon credit
  transfers under the Article 6 of the Paris Agreement (PA4); the adoption of the Sustainable Public Procurement (SPP)
  Guide, to gradually incorporate environmental and social sustainable principles in standard bidding documents of
  targeted sectors, including gender criteria (PA5); the adoption of environmental enforcement guidelines with provisions
  for (i) applying the polluter pays principle with rules-based, progressive sanctions, (ii) using remote, digital information
  tools, and (iii) fostering cooperation among public agencies and citizen-driven accountability (PA6); the adoption of the
  National Air Quality Management Plan, ordering a set of coordinated multi-sector actions to reach WHO Interim Target 1
  for annual PM2.5 (PA7); the adoption of the Energy Efficiency Labeling for Appliances Regulations (EELAR) and the
  Building Energy Efficiency and Environment Regulation (BEEER) (PA8); the adoption of a formula-based and market-linked
  periodic automatic price adjustment system for diesel, heavy fuel oil and octane to ensure cost reflective fuel prices and
  to avoid subsidies (PA9); and the reinstatement of the mandate of the Bangladesh Energy Regulatory Commission (BERC)
  in end-user electricity tariff setting (PA10).

  These policy actions are part of the government’s broader programmatic policy program, coordinated by the Ministry
  of Finance, which includes ten policy engagements that together aim to support Bangladesh’s transition to green and
  climate resilient development. Altogether, these policy engagements address embedding green and climate resilient
  growth in national planning and budgeting, enhancing local planning and financing of green and climate resilient
  priorities, expanding access to carbon markets, promoting social and environmentally sustainable procurement,
  strengthening environmental governance, improving air quality management across productive sectors, increasing
  energy efficiency, reducing the fiscal costs of the energy sector, and improving the efficiency of water supply and
  sanitation services. For most of the policy tracks, the World Bank and other development partners have been providing
  implementation support to the involved agencies through investment credits and technical assistance.
