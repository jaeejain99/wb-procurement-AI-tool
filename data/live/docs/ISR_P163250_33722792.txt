 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Overall project implementation is supervised by a Project Management Unit (PMU) within PKSF. Progress towards achieving the Project

  Development Objective (PDO) “to increase the adoption of environmentally sustainable practices by targeted microenterprises” is on track. PKSF

  has initiated the introduction of environmentally sustainable practices in the overall PKSF microenterprise (ME) program of 30 priority sub-

  sectors/clusters. As of the mid of September 2021, SEP I had introduced environmentally sustainable practices in 30 priority sub-sectors/clusters,

  15 of which were also provided with common services that target MEs’ productivity. Beyond the target of 25,000 MEs to reach, 41,659 MEs (among

  which 83% are female) had signed loan agreements with POs under the project which is significantly 66% higher than the cumulative target of year

  3 (25,000), and 55% of MEs served reported being satisfied with the project interventions (57% of them owned by female). 69.72% MEs adopted at

  least one Environmentally Sustainable Practices (ESP) for over 12 months and 79% were willing to continue the adoption of ESP in the future.

  Sector wise, most of the fund allocated under SEP went to Livestock, Horticulture, Aquaculture, and Food Processing, together amounting to 69%

  of the total fund allocation.

  The PKSF task team has finalized the communication strategy, incorporating comments from the Bank. The partner organizations have a dedicated

  communication professional for all the sub-projects are on board. Since the last mission, both national and local media covered several events and

  project activities. COVID created the incentives to promote commercialization strategies among MEs. Seven e-platforms were developed, 239

  Facebook pages were developed by MEs for product commercialization, and linkages have been developed with Departments of Environment,

  Agriculture, DLS, Finance, BSTI, SGS etc. to ensure a common branding approach and consistency for all social media and print communication

  among the project and sub-projects. Moreover, the PKSF Task team will regularly monitor the outreach and impact of aggregate online and social

  media presence.
