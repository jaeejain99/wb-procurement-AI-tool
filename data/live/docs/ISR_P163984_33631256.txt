 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  GESTIP has made clear progress in advancing project activities. Since April 2021, the PIU worked with government agencies to develop a capacity

  building program, and the project Steering Committee met and approved the inclusion of new technical assistance (TA) activities. The Project will be

  restructured to update the M&E framework now that the new activities have been approved by the Steering Committee. However, project delays

  remain largely due to the time taken to complete selection of firms and commence activities, presenting a risk to disbursing full allocation of project.

  GESTIP activities are aimed at strengthening the capacity of the energy sector to implement sector reforms and improve energy sector planning

  and coordination in Ghana; the activities are essential to the implementation of the Energy Sector Recovery Program (ESRP), dedicated to

  improving sector finances, and are also key inputs to the forthcoming Program for Results operation under preparation.
