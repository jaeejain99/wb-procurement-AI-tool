1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 31-Oct-2023)
   The Project Development Objective is to provide consumption support to vulnerable households with pregnant women
   and mothers of children under 4 years of age and increase knowledge of nurturing care services that contribute to early
   childhood development (ECD).
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project continues to make steady progress toward its development objectives, with the Project Management,
  Monitoring, and Cooridnation Unit now largely functional and key recruitment completed. As of November 2025,
  disbursement stands at US$76.66 million (35.3%), including US$28.4 million linked to achieved Performance -Based
  Conditions (PBCs). Since last reporting period, among others, the project has completed the training and orientation on
  MCBP operational guidelines. During the Implementation Support Mission (October- November 2025), the World Bank
  and the Ministry of Women and Children Affairs focused on progress towards PBC targets in FY2026, which include
  increasing coverage of Mother and Child Benefit Programme (MCBP); ensuring service utilization by women, and
  conducting process reviews in sample locations.
