1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 24-Mar-2023)
   The proposed Program Development Objective (PDO) is to increase equitable access and improve the quality and
   governance of school education in Nepal.

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   As the program enters its third year of implementation, key activities are progressing, including the Recovery and
   Accelerated Learning (ReAL) plan, integrated curriculum, mentoring program, pro-poor scholarships, and monitoring of
   minimum enabling conditions for early childhood development centers. Early evaluations and monitoring efforts have
   identified areas needing improvement to address implementation challenges. In preparation for the upcoming MTR
   scheduled for April 2025, the government, Joint Financing Partners under the Sector-Wide Approach, and other
   development partners will conduct an in-depth review of these challenges and implement necessary adjustments during
   the MTR.
