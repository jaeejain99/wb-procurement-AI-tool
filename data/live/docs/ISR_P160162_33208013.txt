 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The project has financed the construction of two pre-schools in Huong Son and Phan Dinh in June 2021, in time for the new school year; and is on

  schedule for constructing Dan bridge, to be handed over by August 2021. Further, consultations have taken place with expected beneficiaries on

  project interventions. Delays have occurred to date due to lengthy government approval processes, which the client has committed to expedite in

  the coming months.
