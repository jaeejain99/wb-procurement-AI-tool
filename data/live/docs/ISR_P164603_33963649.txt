 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The Ghana Productive Safety Net Project (GPSNP) has generally had successful implementation. Especially during the peak of the COVID-19, the

  project's relevance was increased because it was able to leverage and adapt existing systems built to provide additional financing to Livelihood

  Empowerment Against Poverty (LEAP) beneficiaries and new one-time funding to additional 111,000 poor and vulnerable individuals, as well as

  continue to provide short-term labor in a safe and flexible manner for public works beneficiaries. A few main challenges that slowed down

  implementation are as follows: (i) break in programming twice due to administrative management changes, as a result of political transitions; (ii)

  slowness in approvals at the implementing agencies for finances needed for implementation and procurements; (iii) Government's inability to pay

  LEAP cash grants on time, thereby reducing the rating of Counterpart Financing; and (iv) pull out of the Foreign Commonwealth Development

  Organization (FCDO formerly DFID) financing (almost US$9 million) as a result of economic constraints due to the COVID-19 pandemic.

  However, project implementation significantly progressed since the Mid-term Review Mission in October 2021. Disbursements are now at 96

  percent, and the project will close out with 100 percent disbursement.

  The following are the GPSNP's outcomes at the end of the project: The project surpassed its target for all but one PDO indicator as follows: (i)

  Percent of beneficiaries who initiated or expanded a household enterprise at 89.3 percent surpassing the target of 70 percent; (ii) Average annual

  earnings of unskilled workers in LIPWs (cedi) at 1078.56 surpassing the target of 600; (iii) Percent of respondents reporting that the assets (sub-

  projects) created through LIPW improved their livelihoods at 99.5 percent against a target of 70 percent (iv) Percent of beneficiaries who are

  identified as extremely poor according to targeting criteria at 77.1 percent against a target of 70 percent and Percent of LIPW payments and LEAP

  grants made on time to beneficiaries at 35 percent below the target of 75 percent. Overall rating of the GPSNP is Satisfactory.
