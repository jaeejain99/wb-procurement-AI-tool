 Implementation Status and Key Decisions





  The project has been implemented for two and a half years and the mid-term review (MTR) was conducted in October-November 2022. As the MTR

  results showed, the project development objectives remain highly relevant and are kept unchanged. The project is progressing considerably well

  towards achieving the project objectives and committed results. The mid-term targets of most of the project development and intermediate results

  indicators have been achieved except the utilization of health care services at the commune health stations (CHSs) in the project provinces mainly
