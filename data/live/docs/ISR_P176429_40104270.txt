1. OBJECTIVE
1.1 Development Objective
Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 19-Dec-2024)
The Project Development Objectives (PDOs) are to (i) increase access to safely managed water supply in Chattogram; and
(ii) improve operational performance and financial sustainability of Chattogram Water Supply and Sewerage Authority
(CWASA).

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
Despite some delays in implementation, the project continues to progress toward achieving its development objective and remains
a high priority for key stakeholders. Current efforts are focused on finalizing procurement packages for major works and
consultancy services. The Project Management Unit has been strengthened through the appointment of key personnel, including
the Project Director, Deputy Project Director, and fiduciary and procurement staff.
Civil works are expected to commence by March 2027. In parallel, key institutional support consultancy services will be launched.
Together, these activities are expected to support expanded access to water supply and sanitation services and promote more
sustainable service delivery.

4.2 MPA Program Development Objective
Original MPA Program Development Objective (Approved as part of (Seq. 0) package on )
To (i) improve access to safely-managed water supply and sanitation services, (ii) reduce pollution in rivers and canals,
and (iii) enhance resilience to flood risks in selected urban areas.
