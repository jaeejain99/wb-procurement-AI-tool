3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The Program supports the Government of Bangladesh in improving access to and use of quality health and nutrition services and in building a more resilient health system in selected areas. The Program became effective in June 2026, and an initial advance of US$96.3 million was disbursed. In September 2026, the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, its implementing directorates, and the Implementation Monitoring and Evaluation Division (IMED), which will act as the independent verification agency, agreed on how Program results will be measured, reported, and verified. The
Government is now compiling the results achieved since Program preparation, including in 2025, and will submit them to
IMED for independent verification. Once verified, these results will form the basis for results-based disbursements under the Program.
