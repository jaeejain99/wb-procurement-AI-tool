1. OBJECTIVE
1.1 Development Objective
Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 02-Jun-2022)
The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to strengthen sector institutional capacity for service delivery under federalism and increase access to improved water supply and sanitation services in the participating municipalities.
Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
Yes
Current Development Objective (Approved as part of Restructuring package Seq. 2 on 01-Sep-2025)
The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to strengthen the sector's institutional capacity for service delivery under federalism and to provide safely managed water supply services in participating municipalities.

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The Project implementation has improved over the past 6 months, with 30 contracts awarded and under implementation. Progress on Bheri Pumping Package 1 has accelerated, reaching 30% physical progress. One contract
(Bheri Pumping Package 2) remains under procurement, with any further delays risking the ability to complete the works within the project timeline and the Project achieving its PDO.
