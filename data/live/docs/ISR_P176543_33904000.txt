 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To expand access to broadband in Project areas, to improve the capacity of individuals to engage in the digital economy, and to enhance the
  foundations for digital government.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The progress towards achievement of the Project Development Objective (PDO) is Satisfactory given that there are no material risks currently to
  undermine achievement of the PDO. The Implementation Progress is Satisfactory because the key activities related to signing and effectiveness of
  the Legal Agreements are progressing well and the Project implementing agencies are undertaking preparatory work for various activities financed
  under the Project.
