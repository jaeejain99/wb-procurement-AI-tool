 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The WB and Ministry of Industry and Trade agreed that the Industrial University of Ho Chi Minh City (IUH) would withdraw from the project. The

  project is therefore being restructured to include the cancellation of IUH activities and funding, cancellation of unused VAT as well as the adjustment

  of IUH’s related results indicators and targets. The four remaining implementing agencies are VNUA, HUST, PMB and NEU. The project

  restructuring is expected to be approved by the Government and the World Bank by the end of June 2021. Project implementation has improved

  with major construction and laboratory equipment contracts awarded. The feasibility study reports for the higher education accreditation

  management software and the HEMIS have been completed, laying the technical foundation for detailed design and development

  phases. However, the progress varies between different implementing agencies. Disbursement is currently only 11.58 percent. This ratio

  will increase by the end of the calendar year when ongoing contracts come due.
