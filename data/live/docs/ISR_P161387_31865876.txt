 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  There has been significant reform effort since the last mission in June 2019, in areas related to the achievement of GESDeK results. Notable

  progress has been made in strengthening revenue projections in advance of the 2020/21 budget process and legislative changes have been made

  to align to the approval of the Finance Bill with the Appropriation Bill. A Roadmap and User Requirements for e-Procurement are nearing

  completion. The problems with bank auto-reconciliation have been resolved enabling the balancing of financial assets and liabilities in financial

  statements. The Office of the Auditor General has prepared audits of 2018/19 financial statements in line with its improved methodology, however

  the achievement of its DLIs are at risk due to the delay to the appointment of the Auditor General. A task force has been formed to take the budget

  transparency agenda forward and system functionality developed to capture information on service facilities in Hyperion. In other areas such as

  Public Investment Management and Human Resources, the mission noted that limited progress had been made towards the achievement of DLRs

  by the time of the Mission.







  Overall progress in actual achievement of results has been limited, with only two additional Disbursement Linked Results (DLRs) being achieved,

  subject to verification, since the last ISR. The first was achieved in the area of cash management (DLR 2.3a), where an aggregate cash plan and

  consistent borrowing plan was developed and approved by the newly formed Cash Management Committee and Principal Secretary. The second

  related to internal audit (DLR 6.2b), where the Internal Audit Department has completed its Quality Assurance framework alongside the manuals it

  had developed. Seven DLRs (IDA) valued at US$22.5m planned for achievement by June 2020 cannot be achieved, and they can no longer be

  achieved because they were time bound and dependent on the implementation of reform steps which were delayed. Overall this takes the total

  achieved DLRs to ten, with a value attached of US$14.5m. The original envisaged value of DLIs to be achieved by June 2019 was US$44m and

  US$78m by June 2020. The independent verification agent (IVA) has been appointed and due to complete its verification by March 13,

  2020. [DMN1]







  Nineteen DLRs valued at US$ 41m [LMM2] (IDA) can be achieved by June 2020 in time for the next wave of DLI verification, provided agreed follow

  up actions and Government decisions take place on time . The government team has expressed its commitment to focus on the identified actions

  aimed at fast-tracking the achievement of these results. The upcoming Mid-Term Review (MTR), in the first half of 2020/21 will provide an

  opportunity to assess progress in the achievement of the results, and adjust the program focus going forward.

  A final important development is that the PFMR Strategy for 2018 to 2023 has been approved by the Joint PFM Steering Committee. The

  innovative framework for translating reform efforts into results which help unblock the constraints to public investment and service delivery, to which

  GESDeK is being aligned, is now formally in place. PFMR Strategy Results Teams have replaced the GESDEK results teams. The PFMR

  Secretariat has established results team focal points and the GESDeK coordinator will be the focal point of other aspects of program management.

  It is important that the PFMR secretariat continues to invest in supporting Results Teams and ensures they meet regularly and report on progress.
