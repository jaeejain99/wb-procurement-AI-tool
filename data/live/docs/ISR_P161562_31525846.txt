 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Linked to the Cat DDO Pillar 1, the World Bank leveraged Technical Assistance (TA) resources (US$ 400K) from the African, Caribbean, and

  Pacific (ACP) – European Union (EU) Natural Disaster Risk Reduction (NDRR) Program for “Strengthening the Government of Kenya’s (GoK)’s

  institutional, planning and policy frameworks to manage climate and disaster risk”. Running from April 2019 to February 2020, the Bank-Executed

  grant is financing activities in four components: (i) mainstreaming DRM; (ii) enhancing emergency response capacity; (iii) strengthening the legal

  framework for DRM; and (iv) fortifying the regulatory framework for the built environment in Kenya. For carrying out activities under components

  (i), (ii) and (iii), the Japanese firm Nippon Koei Co., LTD was hired from June 2019 to February 2020 and is working with the National Disaster

  Operations Center (NDOC) and National Drought Management Authority (NDMA) as the main Government Stakeholders. Nippon Koei’s

  consultancy activities include:

  i. updating the methodology that has been used by the National Drought Management Authority (NDMA) for the development of County Multi-

  Hazard Risk Profiles,

  ii. revising the National Emergency Response Plan (2009),

  iii. preparing a Guidance Note for the formulation of County Level Emergency Operation Plans, and,

  iv. supporting the advancement of an approved Disaster Risk Management (DRM) Legal Framework, i.e., the DRM Policy and Act, through the

  National Assembly.
