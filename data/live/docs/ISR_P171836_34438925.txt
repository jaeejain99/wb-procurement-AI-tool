1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 29-Feb-2024)
   The objective of the Project is to enhance inclusive connectivity, efficiency, resilience, and safety of the selected
   provincial and local road network and strengthen the capacity of the federal government, Participating Provinces, and
   Participating Local Levels.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project was approved on February 29, 2024, and the loan was made effective on October 8, 2024. The Project Launch
  workshop was held on November 21, 2024 in Kathmandu in which the memorandum of understanding between the
  three tiers of the participating government i.e. federal government, three participating provinces (Madhesh,
  Sudarpaschim and Karnali) and the participating local levels was also signed.
  The first Implementation Support and Review for the Project will be conducted in the month of January, 2025.
  Meanwhile, the project is progressing well with initiating the tender process for five roads (two each in Sudarpaschim and
  Madhesh province and one in Karnali province). The preparatory work for remaining activities is under progress.
