4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Project Implementation Units (PIUs) across all six implementing agencies have been established and are fully operational. The Economic Relations
  Division is facilitating and coordinating actions across all six PIUs for the Project through a Central Coordination Unit.
  The overall implementation progress (IP) rating is upgraded to Satisfactory from Moderately Satisfactory due to steady improvements in results and
  disbursements. For example, under Component 1 (Building Community Resilience and Self-Reliance), project activities implemented by Department of
  Disaster Management (DDM) have provided services and livelihoods to 500,579 displaced people and 19,785 host community members. The
  Employment Generation Program for the Poorest Plus (EGPP+) has generated 535,518 person-days of work for host communities—reaching 42% of
  the four-year goal in the first year. Furthermore, under Component 4 (Healthier Communities), the results highlight rising coverage of Pentavalent
  vaccination for children (among the Displaced Rohingya Population/DRP) and iron and folate supplementation for adolescent girls (both among DRP
  and host community). Finally, under Component 5 (Gender-Based Violence/GBV Response and Prevention), the Project has supported over 175,000
  visits by women and girls at Women Friendly Spaces in the camps for integrated GBV response and Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights services;
  25,008 vulnerable people supported for livelihoods via Women Led Community Centers; 20,647 Rohingya reached through Social Behavioral
  Communication Campaign; 16,198 engaged via Start Awareness Support and Action (SASA) tools; and 11.85% of GBV response service seekers were
  referred to appropriate levels of care. Results are being further verified to prevent double-counting as an individual can recieve multiple benefits from
  Project interventions. Revisions will be reflected in the next Implementation Status and Results Report. Also, the project has reached cumulative
  disbursement of over US$78.07 million (22 percent of net IDA commitment) as of September 13, 2025.
