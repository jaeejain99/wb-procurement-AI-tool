1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 31-Oct-2023)
   The Project Development Objective is to provide consumption support to vulnerable households with pregnant women
   and mothers of children under 4 years of age and increase knowledge of nurturing care services that contribute to early
   childhood development (ECD).
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project continues to make strong progress toward achieving its development objectives. As of April 2025, over 1.6
  million pregnant women and mothers of young children have been reached with cash transfers under the Mother and
  Child Benefit Programme (MCBP). During the Implementation Support Mission (April 16–30, 2025), the Bank task team
  and the Ministry of Women and Children Affairs worked closely to sustain implementation momentum, including
  onboarding key project staff, fast-tracking procurement of critical packages, and accelerating training and orientation
  activities on MCBP operational guidelines to achieve Performance-Based Condition (PBC) 2.1. Efforts are also actively
  underway to strengthen MIS functionality, further supporting the achievement of PBC 3.1, which targets a 10% expansion
  in beneficiary coverage over the previous fiscal year.
