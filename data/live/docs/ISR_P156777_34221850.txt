 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  KUSP implementation progress is on track to achieve the project development objective. Apart from the 59 beneficiary municipalities, KUSP has

  catalyzed the formation of an additional 18 municipalities. In terms of urban infrastructure and services, in total 370 infrastructure investments have

  been implemented and are at different levels of operationalization. Of these, 67 percent correspond to connectivity and mobility infrastructure, 17

  percent are socio economic infrastructure including markets and public parks, 9 percent are storm water drainage infrastructure, and 7 percent are

  disaster management and solid waste infrastructure. As of September 2023 reporting period, over 88 percent of these investments are completed or

  substantially complete and in use, with 95%, 92% and 70% completed and operation under FY 18/19 , FY 19/20 and FY 20/21 financing tranches

  respectively. Only 33 infrastructure investments are ongoing, with half being over 90% complete and all of the ongoing works above 50%

  completion. Most works are expected to be completed by December 31, 2023, with commitments from County Governments to finalize any

  outstanding works as soon as possible, as part of the Government Program this operation supports.
