1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 01-Jun-2022)
   The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to strengthen sector institutional capacity for service delivery under
   federalism and increase access to improved water supply and sanitation services in the participating municipalities.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The major milestone of awarding the Bheri Pumping Sub-Project Package 1 (BP1) contract has been achieved. This is
  the first contract awarded at the municipal level and the largest contract planned under the project. This was a key
  project milestone, contributing close to 30 percent of IDA commitments.
  There are ongoing delays in other municipalities. All municipalities should award their first water supply contracts by
  December 30, 2024, and complete the first public toilets by June 30, 2025. Funds should be allocated to municipalities
  that demonstrate satisfactory progress.
