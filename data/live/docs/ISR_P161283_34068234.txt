 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The project is progressing considerably well towards achieving the project objectives and committed results. Most of the project development and

  intermediate results indicators have been achieved. However, two indicators on utilization of health care services at the commune health stations

  (CHSs) and health insurance coverage have not recovered from the COVID-19 pandemic, mainly due to the policy allowing patients to by-pass

  grassroots health facilities and reduced subsidy of health insurance premium for the near poor. The health sectors and provinces are fully aware of

  these issues and have had concrete plans to address.
