  Comments
  M&E is rated Moderately Satisfactory. The Government has reported on 12 of 14 results indicators. Two indicators
  remain unreported or incomplete: (i) nationwide data on forest area covered by new and updated community forest
  management plans compliant with Forest Regulation 2022 (Indicator 6A), which is expected in 2026; and (ii) data on the
  share of annual income of forest user groups spent on women’s empowerment and female entrepreneurship (Indicator
  6B), which is not yet available. The ICR will incorporate these data if available in early 2026. The rating reflects partial
  coverage and the need to strengthen data collection and consolidation across agencies.

  The Program Management rating is informed by the Ministry of Finance’s continued performance in coordinating the
  participating line ministries and delivering the reform program across five governments since the start of the DPC series.

 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
Progress toward achievement of Project Development Objective is Moderately Unsatisfactory. GRID DPC-2 was
disbursed on April 25, 2025, after being declared effective on March 17, 2025. The Government demonstrated
sustained commitment to the policy program across five consecutive administrations with no policy reversals.
However, the targets of only six of the 14 results indicators have been achieved. The targets of three additional
results indicators (Indicators 6A, 9A and 9B) are likely to be achieved within one year of the program's early
closure, but the likelihood of achieving the targets of the remaining five results indicators remains low. The low
achievement of results indicators stems in part from political upheaval in September 2025 and the subsequent
formation of an interim government, which delayed critical approvals for reforms already technically developed,
such as the sixth amendment to the National Parks and Wildlife Act (2030 Bikram Sambat). Moreover, several
approved frameworks await operationalization, including draft SESA guidelines that have yet to be applied within a
project (Indicator 4) and shock-responsive social protection standards that require regulatory amendments to SSA
and NEP guidelines, system modifications, and development of a social registry before systematic application
during disasters (Indicator 7). Additional challenges include the slow pace of municipal-level land use classification
approvals (Indicator 2) and the absence of robust data collection and monitoring systems to track community
forest user group spending on women empowerment activities (Indicator 6B). The National Parks and Wildlife
Conservation Regulation must still be developed, endorsed and operationalized (Indicator 5). The early closure of
the programmatic series due to the cancellation of GRID DPC-3 will significantly impact the achievement of End-of-
Program Results Indicators and thereby affect the performance ratings of the overall program.


The third operation was cancelled following a May 15, 2025 letter from the Ministry of Finance. Despite this, the
Government delivered several policy actions originally anticipated for the third operation, including new standards
for shock-responsive social protection and water supply and sanitation regulations. The close of DPC-2 marked the
end of the programmatic GRID DPC financing.
