3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The Government of Bangladesh has decided to prepare Development Project Proposals (DPPs) for high-priority thematic
   areas as an alternative to approving the Fifth Health Sector Program. In line with this, the Ministry of Health and Family
   Welfare (MoHFW) developed two DPPs to cover the areas under this Program for Results operation: one for the
   Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) and the other for the Directorate General of Family Planning (DGFP) of the
   MoHFW. The DPPs have been approved by ECNEC in January 2026. The operation can now be restructured and signing of
   the financing agreement and commencement of implementation is expected within the current calendar quarter.
