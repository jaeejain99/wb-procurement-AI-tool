4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The project achieved Effectiveness on December 8, 2025, following fulfillment of all effectiveness conditions. Since the national elections in February 2026, implementation has accelerated and the project has progressed into the detailed design phase.
The Government of Bangladesh and the CPA have engaged a transaction advisor to support transaction structuring and procurement of international terminal operator(s), who will be responsible for the development and operation of the terminal(s) under a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) arrangement.
