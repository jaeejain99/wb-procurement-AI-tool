3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The Mid-Term Review for the Program took place in November 2025. The review found that Program Development
Objectives are on track toward end-of-Program targets, despite recognized implementation and coordination constraints.
Progress is strongest in the quality of care and emergency preparedness activities, while implementation of Electronic
Medical Records and health insurance enrollment is delayed. There are also implementation bottlenecks, including
uneven federal-provincial coordination, weak inter-agency follow-up, slightly delayed fiduciary reporting, and capacity
constraints, particularly in Gandaki. The Program results remain achievable, but will require stronger implementation
discipline, faster resolution of delayed disbursement-linked results, and closer coordination across the Ministry of Health
and Food Safety, the Health Insurance Boards, and provinces.
