 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  A Mid-term Review Mission was held for the GPSNP from September 27 through October 6, 2021 to conduct a thorough review of project

  implementation status at mid-term. A field mission was also undertaken between October 10 and 14, 2021.

  Disbursement rates are high, primarily due to frontloading of funds for cash transfers during the peak of the COVID-19 pandemic. The project was

  therefore projected to close by December 2021, but due to implementation challenges at MoGCSP, the projected closing date is now June 30,

  2022.

  The project has made significant progress since inception, particularly with effective roll out of the LIPW component which has exceeded its target

  of reaching 30,000 beneficiaries, by reaching 34,062, and implementing physical works at 344 sites in 80 districts. The component also continues to

  be strong in its social inclusion agenda with over 60% female participation, the existence of on-site creches at all sites; and signing of GBV code of

  COVID-19 pandemic, given investments in establishing a strong SP system, the GPSNP was instrumental in providing rapid support to poor and

  vulnerable people through collecting data on the urban poor using the GNHR; and paying cash transfers through the LEAP payment and

  reconciliation system, including adapting payment modalities by introducing mobile money transfers.

  Despites strides made across components, at mid-term a number of major challenges that risk the achieve met of the PDO, are documented: (i)

  continued delays by Government in disbursement of cash transfers; Delays in payments have been noted since October 2020; (ii) reduction in

  availability of GPSNP grant funding towards cash transfers due to the FCDO’s inability to provide continued funding towards the project; (iii) General

  delays in approvals for implementing program activities at MoGCSP; and (iv) delayed initiation of 2020 Financial Audit at the MLGDRD. The MTR

  mission reviewed these challenges and identified mitigations to ensure that the project can meet its development objective. These were

  documented in the mission's Aide Memoire.
