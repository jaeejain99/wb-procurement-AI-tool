 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The US$35 million Credit to finance the Public Sector Reform for Results Project (PSRRP) was approved by the Board of Executive Directors of the

  World Bank on October 23, 2018. The project was declared effective on May 8, 2019. The PSRRP supports Ghana’s National Public Sector Reform

  Strategy, 2018-2023. It helps Selected Entities (SEs) improve efficiency and accountability in delivery of key public services to citizens and firms,

  such as issuance and renewal of passports, vehicle registration and environmental permits, and registration of births and deaths. To incentivize

  these sixteen entities to improve, Performance-Based Conditions (PBCs) will reward entities which achieve agreed results and improve public

  accountability. Implementation support will be provided by the World Bank to strengthen the entities’ capacity to achieve the agreed results.

  The Project was successfully restructured in March 2022, to modify and simplify the PBCs. The Project is now driven by a simplified, outcome-

  oriented matrix of seven (7) PBCs. Three SEs – the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), Ghana Immigration Service (GIS) and Ministry of

  The Progress against most PBCs is on track; however, the overall disbursement remains low at US$ 5.92 million, or 18.5% of the total commitment
