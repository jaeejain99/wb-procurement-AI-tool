Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 02-Jul-2024)

in selected clusters, and (ii) strengthen urban management capacity in selected urban centers.

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The project has encountered significant delays, primarily due to the prolonged period for the appointment of a
substantive Project Director. As a result, procurement progress has been slower than anticipated. It is too early to
measure progress toward development outcomes, but the project remains focused on establishing the planning
foundations – particularly Cluster and Pourashava Development Plans - that will guide strategic investments for nodal
cities and their clusters in the next phase.
