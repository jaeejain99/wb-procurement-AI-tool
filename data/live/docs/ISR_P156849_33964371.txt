 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The Project is making progress to achieve its project development objective (PDO) which is to improve research, teaching, and institutional

  capacity at selected autonomous universities and strengthen the national higher education management system. The three outcomes indicators

  have surpassed the end of project targets. Similarly, seven out of ten intermediate indicators have achieved/exceeded their targets. All 42

  procurement contracts, with a total value of about US$105 million have been awarded. The project’s activities related to training, research, and

  accreditation continue to be implemented well by both the Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST) and Vietnam National University for

  Agriculture (VNUA); while the National Economics University (NEU) has completed all the activities and inaugurated the e-library in November

  2022. The project restructuring to cancel the Industry University of Hochiminh City was completed in December 2022.
