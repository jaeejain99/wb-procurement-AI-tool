 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 22-Sep-2023)
   The Program Development Objective (PDO) is to improve student learning and retention, and build system resilience in

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   Program results and overall implementation progress are on track despite disruption in academic activities in school due
   to recent flood and school closures. To mitigate these challenges, the task team is providing technical support to the MoE
   to ensure learning continuity and timely achievement of program results
   Program has successfully disbursed SDR 1.51 million equivalent to US$ 2 million, as an advance for the IPF component in
   the designated account on June 3, 2024. This followed an earlier disbursement of SDR 52.125 million equivalent to
   approximately US$ 68.9 million, as a PforR advance on May 9, 2024. The total disbursement under the credit now stands
   at US$ 70.9 million, representing 24% of the credit
   A new Deputy Project Director has been appointed, joining the Project Director (PD) and other key personnel within the
   Project Implementation Unit (PIU). Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) was signed between the MoE and the
   Implementation Monitoring and Evaluation Division (IMED) of the Planning Commission for the verification of results
   under the program. Ministry of Education (MoE) has approved an operations manual as per requirement of the legal
   agreement
