 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Overall Progress. Although substantial delays during the initial years of implementation (including a long lag from approval to effectiveness) will

  likely require adjustment of some project activities and output targets, current implementation performance is promising. The provinces have made

  significant progress in coastal forest planting and rehabilitation with the cumulative completion rate (area completed/proposed revised target) having

  surpassed 80 percent by April 30, 2023. Quang Ninh, Hai Phong, Nghe An, Ha Tinh, and Thua Thien Hue provinces are planning to conduct

  additional planting in 2023 to cover the areas carried over from the 2022 planting season due to technical and climatic reasons. On the other hand,

  progress under Component 3 (small-scale infrastructure, technology, and livelihood subprojects) still varies considerably across the provinces.

  Strong community engagement has been observed at the project sites, including by local villagers contracted to undertake planting and tending

  after receiving technical training. About 40-50 villagers per commune are involved in sandy soil and mangrove planting and rehabilitation at each

  visited site. There is also evidence that farmers in the project area have acquired new skills and increased income from non-timber forest products

  as a result of better forest management. Communities are also beginning to benefit from improved forest protection infrastructure such as roads that
