 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  This ISR is based on the implementation support mission undertaken from April 12 to 17, 2023. The project was approved by the board in Feb 2017

  and became effective in May 2017. A project restructuring was undertaken in October 2020 and is now scheduled to close on June 30, 2023.

  In terms of project results, there are several notable achievements both at the county level and the national level.

            Kenya Climate Smart Agriculture Project (P154784)







          A total of 771,000 farmers across 24 counties are receiving a range of benefits from input support to access to modern agriculture

           technologies, innovations and management practices (TIMPs) to access to markets and access a range of agro-weather and market

           advisories.

           144 Community Driven Development Committees (CDDCs – One in every project ward) who will be transformed into Savings and Credit

           Cooperatives (SACCOs) and 199 Producer Organizations have been established and registered with dedicated bank accounts.

          190 sub project investments (water related investments, market infrastructure related investments) have been taken up for implementation

           under the project. Of these, 143 sub projects have already completed implementation and the rest 47 sub projects are at 85% completion.

          Big Data platform established at the Kenya Agriculture and Livestock Research Organization (KALRO) with the support from the project

           has been established as a result of which all project farmers are receiving integrated agro weather and market advisories.

  Given the continued good progress since the last implementation support mission across all components both the PDO and IP ratings are being

  retained as "Satisfactory".
