 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The project successfully achieved its PDO in October 2023, surpassing the targets two months ahead of the scheduled project closure. By

  December 12, 2023, all of the PDO-level indicators had been met or exceeded, with outcomes for user satisfaction significantly exceeding their

  targets, e.g. the percentage of users satisfied with newly built or improved roads was 97.0 vs a target of 80.0; and the percentage of users satisfied

  with the new or improved public spaces is likewise 97 vs a target of 80.0. In both cases the share of female and bottom 40% users also exceeded

  the respective targets. All of 16 intermediate indicators had likewise been met. The project has facilitated the rehabilitation and construction of two

  pre-primary schools, the installation of a 5-kilometer stormwater and wastewater drainage system in the urban core, and the enhancement of urban

  connectivity through the construction of four main bridges and the creation of a 14-kilometer road linking the core urban area with the under-

  development zone. All completed infrastructure is operational, making substantial contribution to the resilience and accessibility of the urban

  infrastructural services in Thái Nguyên city.
