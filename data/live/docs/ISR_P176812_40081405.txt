 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Word Bank approved US$650 million USD on June 28, 2024 to finance the Bay Terminal Marine Infrastructure
  Development Project. The project aims to enable the mobilization of private investment for the primary gateway port of
  Bangladesh and increase its operation efficiency. It comprises two major components: (i) support port marine civil
  infrastructure, equipment, and navigation system development; and (ii) support port efficiency improvements and
  capacity development.
  The project loan agreements were declared effective on December 8, 2025. The Government of Bangladesh and the CPA
  have also engaged a transaction advisor to support the recruitment of international terminal operator(s), who will be
  responsible for the development and operation of the terminal(s) under a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) arrangement.
