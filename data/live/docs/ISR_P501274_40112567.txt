4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
A Mid-Term Review of the Host and Rohingya Enhancement of Lives Project (HELP) was conducted in May and June 2026,
jointly with the Economic Relations Division, the four line ministries, and the six implementing agencies. The review
reaffirmed the continued relevance of the Project Development
At the mid-point, the project has established a sound implementation foundation and is delivering measurable results.
Project Implementation Units across all six agencies are staffed and operational, and specialized consultants, including
design, supervision, and monitoring and evaluation support, are mobilized. Contracts have been signed across all
components, and civil works are progressing in water supply, sanitation, and rural infrastructure. As of the mission,
approximately 253,816 people, including about 131,968 women, had been provided with improved services and
livelihood support against an end-target of 645,000. The afforestation program has exceeded its end-target, with about
5,070 hectares achieved against a target of 3,500 hectares, contributing to ecosystem restoration.
