 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The project completed the construction of two pre-schools in Huong Son and Phan Dinh in June 2021 and both have been put into operation and

  have achieved their intended results with respect to improved student to teacher ratios. The construction of Dan bridge was completed and put into

  service in July 2022 while the upgrading of Dong Bam residential area road to Mo Linh 2 bridge will be completed for turnover in January 2023. All

  project-financed activities are on track to be completed and the project is expected to achieve the targeted project development objectives by the

  project closing date of December 31, 2023.
