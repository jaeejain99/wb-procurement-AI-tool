 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The GoN Council of Ministers declared a State of Disaster Endangered Area in the Sindhupalchowk and Baglung districts from September 22 to

  October 16, 2020, and requested a Cat DDO partial disbursement of US$25 M on September 27, 2020. After assessing the situation and

  confirming that the drawdown condition of a declaration of disaster was met, the Bank confirmed the acceptance of evidences furnished on

  September 30, 2020. The MoF submitted a withdrawal application for the approved amount to the Bank office on October 2, 2020 and it was

  processed and disbursed on the same date.

  A DRM-Health Bank team carried out a virtual Implementation Review of this DPF with Cat DDO on October 12 - 14, 2020, with the main objective

  of assessing the progress towards achieving the results envisaged under the Prior Actions of this operation. The Bank team noted significant

  progress in achieving the results indicators despite the difficult situation due to COVID-19 and the monsoon historic precipitation. The Bank and

  GON teams agreed on key actions to continue working on the Cat DDO results.
