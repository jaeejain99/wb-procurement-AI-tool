 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  KUSP implementation progress is on track to achieve the project development objective. Apart from the 59 beneficiary municipalities, KUSP has

  catalyzed the formation of an additional 17 municipalities of which seven (Elwak, Mwingi, Lietein, Diani, Kitengela, Bondo and Ugunja) have a

  municipal charter gazetted, and urban boards and municipal managers appointed. The remaining 10 have municipal charters gazetted. This

  demonstrates that the Program has had institutional development impacts beyond the targeted 59 established municipalities. In terms of urban

  infrastructure and services, from the three disbursed tranches, 369 infrastructure investments have been implemented and are at different levels of

  operationalization, including connectivity and mobility infrastructure (67 percent), socio economic infrastructure including markets and public parks

  (17 percent), storm water drainage (9 percent) and disaster management and solid waste infrastructure (7 percent). About 96 percent and 87

  percent of the activities have been completed and are operational under the 2018/2019 and 2019/2020 financing tranches respectively. Under the

  last tranche (2020/2021), 65 percent of the activities have been completed.
