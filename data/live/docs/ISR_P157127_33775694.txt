 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Overall Progress. The investment mapping – process involving detailed review and demarcation of the project land areas for new planting,

  rehabilitation and protection was completed in all project provinces, which was approved by the respective Provincial People’s Committees (PPCs)

  in May 2021, thus setting the stage for planting, rehabilitation and protection activities. All provinces except for Hai Phong subsequently initiated

  planting/rehabilitation activities for the 2021 planting season, completing a total of 1,566 ha of planting and/or rehabilitation by the end of December

  2021. There has also been progress in procurement; 33 contracts out of the 51 packages in the agreed procurement plans having been awarded.

  Disbursement remains low at US$ 7.7 million (5.13%) (Client Connection data as of January 18, 2021). This is because the majority of the awarded

  contracts are at technical design stage, and disbursement of these contracts is expected to significantly increase by the end of FY22 when civil

  works start.
