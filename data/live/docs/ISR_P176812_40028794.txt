 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Bangladesh Bay Terminal Marine Infrastructure Development Project (US$650 million) was approved by the World
  Bank in June 2024. The Development Project Proposal was approved by the Government of Bangladesh in April 2024. The
  financing agreement between the World Bank and the Government of Bangladesh was signed in April 2025, followed by
  the project agreement between the World Bank and the Chittagong Port Authority (CPA) in May 2025. The project's
  primary activity is the construction of a breakwater and access channel.
  The Government of Bangladesh and the CPA are currently working to fulfill the following effectiveness conditions ahead
  of the deadline on September 2, 2025:
      • Establishment of a dedicated, full-time Project Implementation Unit (PIU)
      • Signing of the Subsidiary Loan Agreement between CPA and the Government of Bangladesh
      • Finalization of the feasibility study, project procurement strategy, and procurement plan
      • Finalization of the Project Operations Manual
  In parallel, the Government of Bangladesh and the CPA are also in the process of engaging a transaction advisor to
  support the recruitment of international terminal operator(s), who will be responsible for the development and
  operation of the container terminal(s) under a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) arrangement.
