 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The project is progressing well with respect to achievement of all six Project Development Objective (PDO) indicators - (i) reduction of procurement

  time using electronic government procurement (e-GP) has already exceeded the project end target – it takes on an average of 57 days to complete

  procurement against the target of 70 days; (ii) newly selected 916 public sector organizations invited 80% of their procurement through e-GP

  against the project end target of 100%; (iii) citizen monitoring in contract implementation is ongoing in 48 upazilas (sub-districts) against the target of

  45; (iv) development of Implementation Monitoring and Evaluation Division’s (IMED) project management information system (PMIS) for online

  implementation monitoring of projects is finally moving at full swing. All major modules are already developed, and the testing of the system is now

  ongoing with a target to complete the testing phase by May 2022; (v) electronic contract management and payment (e-CMS) module of the e-GP

  has been deployed for country wide implementation by all public agencies in February 2022; and (vi) International Competitive Bidding (ICB)

  module of the e-GP has been developed for the government and development partner funded projects and already piloted successfully for a
