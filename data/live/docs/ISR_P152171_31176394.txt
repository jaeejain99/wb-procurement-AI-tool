 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overall, apart from component 1, there continues to be good progress in the implementation of the project. As a result, the project implementation

  is remains at Satisfactory (S). There have been no additional disbursements since the last ISR, as the previous disbursement was sufficient to fund

  ongoing activities.

  There has been little progress in component 1, and this will be one of the subject's of discussion in the MTR.

  The debt department has conducted a number of training and capacity building activities and as a result has been able to prepare robust Medium

  Term Debt Strategy and Anual Borrowing plan for 2019.

  The full operationalization of the trading room, which is still being finalized, will further improve the availability of real-time information from the

  market and communication with relevant market players. The date for completion was revised to end of June 2019 due to delays in obtaining

  suitable space and setting up of the room at the Ministry of Finance.

  Under component 3 the team has developed the Public Investment Management Operations Manual and associated guidelines and templates. The

  PIP Working Committee has also been established. Moreover, progress was made on key activities including the conduct of a comprehensive

  organizational review and capacity assessment, development of PIM regulations; the design and implementation of a comprehensive and

  continuous training program at relevant national tertiary-level training institutions; preparation of a National Infrastructure Plan (NIP); and

  development of a national M&E system. Here, also, the completion date was revised from the December 2018 to October 2019.

  Under Component 4 the equity study was completed and the recommendations of the Report have been reviewed and submitted to the

  Management of MoF for approval. In the mean time MOF shared outcomes in the study in the State Ownership workshop organized on September

  27 2018. MoF is working on getting approval to allow for full scale dissemination.

  In addition, the Single Entity i.e. State Interests and Governance Authority (SIGA) law was passed by Parliament in May, 2019, enabling progress in

  preparation for its establishment as an institution. Therefore, MOF is now working with relevant institutions in activating all other actions that were

  contingent in the passage of the law. These actions include

       1. Developing the organizational chart, setting up the structure and job descriptions, and supporting the recruitment of qualified staff.

       2. Developing and supporting the implementation of approved policies, guidelines, and tools for the Government as shareholder (for

            example, board nominations framework, performance monitoring system, aggregate annual reporting for the SOE sector as a whole).

       3. Developing and supporting policies, guidelines, and tools for SOEs in its portfolio (such as financial reporting standards for the SOEs to

            adopt and implement, disclosure policies, templates for annual reporting).
