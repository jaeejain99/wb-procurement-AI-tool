4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS





4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project progress is on track and all the planned activities will be completed by the project closing. Almost all project

  development indicators and intermediate results are likely to achieve the targets by the project closing date except two

  PDO indicators on fully vaccination coverage for children under one year and service utilization at commune health

  stations.

  The project will be closed on December 31, 2024. End project evaluation will be conducted from August 2024 and the

  final implementation support mission is scheduled in late October 2024.
