1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 29-Feb-2024)
   The objective of the Project is to enhance inclusive connectivity, efficiency, resilience, and safety of the selected
   provincial and local road network and strengthen the capacity of the federal government, Participating Provinces, and
   Participating Local Levels.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Project implementation is underway, marked by the award of five upgradation contracts covering 60 km and
  totaling US$11.95 million across Madhesh, Sudurpaschim, and Karnali provinces. Draft DPRs for an additional 100
  km of upgradation and 58 km of new connectivity roads are being prepared, along with environmental and social
  instruments, and are expected to be finalized by August 2025. Surveys for periodic maintenance have been
  completed, with cost estimates under finalization for tendering. Preparatory activities for routine maintenance have
  also been initiated.
