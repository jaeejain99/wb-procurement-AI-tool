4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Implementation progress across the project has been slower than envisaged. Tendering for the Fulchari riverbank
  protection is at its final stage with expected contract award in June 2026. The tendering for similar work in Kalihati was
  issued in December 2025, with expected contract award in July 2026. Timely award of contracts is needed so that
  preparatory works can start during the 2026 monsoon season. Under component 3, the insurance trigger design has
  advanced. The insurance product and community protection fund are targeted to achieve operational readiness by June
  2026 to be activated during the 2026 monsoon season.
