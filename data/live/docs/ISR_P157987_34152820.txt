 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project aims to improve the capacity of the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS) to produce quality core statistics and make them accessible
  in a timely manner to policy makers and the public.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Summary of Project Implementation Progress and Key Issues

  Component A. Improving the Coordination and Management of Statistical Activities
     1. Mechanisms to enhance coordination and reporting on HR practices: After receiving comments from technical and validation teams,
        the Twinning Partner (TP) has submitted the final deliverables on: (a) report on MoUs, (ii) report on Administrative Data, (iii) report on
        Functional Review and (iv) report on HR Recruitment. Thus far, TP has organized a workshop with other institutions and held introductory
        meetings from which a draft MoUs’ has been prepared. Response from BBS Wings is required to finalize this activity and deliverable. In
        addition, the process for the review and update of the National Strategy for the Development of Statistics (NSDS) has started. The PIU

             has already hired the local and international consultants, prepared detailed work plan of activities and held several workshops or
             meetings.
      2.     Memorandum of Understanding (MoU): The Twinning Partner has prepared draft MoUs with different data producers. BBS was also
             able to sign and operationalize one MoU with the Finance Division, Ministry of Finance and negotiations are ongoing to achieve the end
             target of signing and operationalization of four MoUs before project closing.
      3.     Training: Twinning Partner (TP) has submitted the materials for the Training on Quality Management. About 20 BBS officials were already
             trained on the approved manual in the first quarter of 2023. Another round of training is scheduled in July 2023. The TP has additionally
             suggested merging similar local trainings to make this process efficient.

  Component B. Developing Human Resources and ICT Infrastructure to Produce and Manage Data
     1. Investing in core skills and competencies: This component partly focuses on the development of Human Resources (HR) and includes
        the drafting of a training policy as well as the full “training engineering cycle” that starts from the identification of training needs and supply,
        the design, and the implementation of a training program. The objective was to develop human resources by investing in core skills and
        competencies. The mission was brought up to speed on the trainings conducted since the September 2022 ISM based on the Training
        needs assessment and inventory of statistical trainings. These include:
              a. Training on Introduction to Time Series Analysis, STATA, and R Software was conducted for 89 specialist staff.
              b. Quantum Index of Industrial Production (QIIP) and Producer Price Index (PPI) training held at the BBS and the field offices
                   for 330 BBS officials.
              c. Twinning Partner provided training on the Fundamental Price Statistics, Health Statistics, and Labor Statistics to enhance
                   the capacity of participants to contribute to the meaningful development of these Statistics, in particular the collection of
                   appropriate and accurate data in line with international standards and the interpretation of statistics obtained considering national
                   policies and circumstances.
              d. A three-day Refresher Training for enumerators was held on HIES and QLFS surveys.
              e. Two batches of 69 officials were provided training on Sampling Technique and Practice by TP.
              f.   A batch of 20 officials was provided training on Statistical Methods for Estimation and Analysis by TP.
              g. Training on Survey Management Design and Operations was provided to 76 BBS officials.
              h. Basic training was provided to 1,539 staff on Computer Literacy and another 1,557 officials were trained over two rounds on
                   Basic Agriculture Training by PIU.
     2. Investments in ICT infrastructure to support data collection and management: The ICT Plan and ICT Roadmap draft has been
        prepared by Twinning Partner (TP) and TP is addressing the comments received from senior management of Statistics and Informatics
        Division (SID). After initial delays procurement of ICT goods and equipment is in progress. The project financing agreement states that all
        project goods are to be procured and purchased within the project period. The procurement process itself is time intensive as it requires
        finding the correct product specifications, installing and setting-up equipment, and commissioning and receiving training from independent
        suppliers. The Project Team is working with PIU and TP to complete the ICT Procurement process on time.
     3. Developing the website: The Twinning Partner received feedback from the Validation Group on the design of the new website and is
        incorporating comments received in the revision process. The main feedback from these meetings "was to restructure the website and to
        include the different BBS Wings in the structure”. PIU will arrange a Management Meeting to discuss and agree on the key changes that
        BBS would like for the website. Twinning Partner will organize website maintenance, handover, and relevant training with experts. They
        will also undertake server hosting during the development phase prior to handover once all changes have been incorporated and
        approved.

  Component C. Improving the Coverage and Quality of Core Statistics Required for Policy Making
     1. User Satisfaction Survey (USS): The Project successfully conducted a User Satisfaction Survey (USS) and the final report disseminated
        on March 15, 2023, at the BBS auditorium. The results indicate that about a third of BBS data users are satisfied with the official data
        produced by the National Statistics Office (NSO).
     2. Progress in implementation of the 2022 Labor Force Survey: The BBS completed full year data collection of the Quarterly Labor Force
        Survey (QLFS) using the Computer-Assisted Personal Interviewing (CAPI) and released the Preliminary Findings of the of the survey on
        March 29, 2023. The second-year data collection of QLFS is ongoing, and already BBS has released the first quarter findings in a
        dissemination event held on May 5, 2023. At the dissemination event decision was made to institutionalize the QLFS and publish results
        regularly according to the survey release calendar.
     3. Progress in implementation of the 2022 Household Income and Expenditure Survey (HIES): BBS HIES Team has completed the
        2022 Household Income and Expenditure Survey (HIES) in December 2022 using Computer-Assisted Personal Interviewing (CAPI). The
        2022 HIES Key Findings Report was released on April 12, 2023.

  Component D. Promoting and Strengthening Access to and the Use of Official Statistics
     1. Documenting statistical activities and providing better access to data: The Twinning Partner (TP) experts continue to provide
        technical support for documentation, microdata anonymization, and making data accessible for further research and analysis. The review
        and finalization of several deliverables have been done, including survey documentation, data visualization, preparation of codes and
        syntax, and micro data anonymization. The TP experts plan to conduct training for BBS Computer Wing staff on the process for data
        documentation, microdata anonymization and making the data accessible. A draft Dissemination Policy has been prepared and is
        currently under review for completion.
     2. Survey release calendar: The Advanced Survey Release Calendar exists but has not been updated and not on the website. PIU is
        requested to keep the survey release calendar up to date to monitor timely release of survey reports and data. PIU will further coordinate

  Component E. Project Management
     1. Progress in Twinning Partnership (TP) activities. The progress in implementation of the TP contract is satisfactory having successfully
        completed more than half of the deliverables. The TP has completed 22 out of 24 deliverables across the four main project components
        (Component A, B, C and D). Progress was noted on implementation of previous recommendations and the challenges, particularly in
        organizing meetings with different data producers with interest of discussing MoU modalities and protocols. The Twinning Partner Contract
        is currently under consideration for another extension to accommodate the pending pilot surveys and trainings.
