3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The Nepal Quality Health Systems Program-for-Results operation became effective on January 10, 2024. The
   Program includes financing of US$100 million from the International Development Association (IDA), and
   US$3.84 million from the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response (HEPR) Trust Fund to strengthen
   Nepal's efforts to deliver quality healthcare services, ensure financial protection to its citizens, especially to the
   poor and vulnerable, and build resilient public health systems.
   Most of this FY DLRS are expected to be achieved by the end of the fiscal year. Out of seven DRLs planned for
   this FY, five have been achieved as per evidence provided by the MOHP and Provinces during March mission.
   Remaining two are on track but their implementation should be accelerated. Overall Program implementation is
   progressing as planned.
   To enhance the implementation of activities for this fiscal year and next, the MOHP should provide guidance and
   coordination to all implementing agencies, including Gandaki and Koshi Province. Additionally, the MOHP should
   ensure that an appropriate budget is secured for the implementation of these activities.
