The Program Development Objectives are to strengthen labor market relevance and quality of higher education, boost
collaborative research and innovation, and enhance equitable access for underprivileged and disaster affected groups.

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The UGC and participating universities and colleges have made considerable progress in implementing key activities,
including Labor Market Driven Programs (LMDPs), the Entrepreneurship Support Program, collaborative research,
QAA/QE, pro-poor scholarships, and training for faculty and senior management. However, in early May 2026, the
government decided to remove politically appointed high-level public officials, resulting in an abrupt leadership
vacuum at both the UGC and the universities during a critical final phase of program implementation. Although
implementation activities have continued, the resulting institutional uncertainty has slowed decision-making, delayed
key approvals, and disrupted the continuity of some reform activities, particularly those requiring high-level
coordination and policy endorsement. These delays could affect completion of remaining PDO-related activities and
associated disbursements, especially for LMDP monitoring plan, QAA/QE targets, and DLI 6 digitization activities,
unless the restructuring and six-month extension are processed promptly and implementation is closely monitored.
