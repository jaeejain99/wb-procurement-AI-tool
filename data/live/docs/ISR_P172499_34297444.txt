 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 19-Sep-2023)
   The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to increase capacity for flood management and navigation along the Jamuna
   River.

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project implementation for the Jamuna River Sustainable Management Project 1 has commenced. All three DPPs
  have been approved. The Project Directors have been assigned and have commenced their work on the implementation.
  The three implementation agencies are currently in the process of assigning more staff to establish the Project
  Implementation Units and enhance their implementation capacity. Review of designs is ongoing.
