 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The Project has achieved its project development objective (PDO) , which is to improve research, teaching, and institutional capacity at selected

  autonomous universities and strengthen the national higher education management system. The three outcomes indicators have surpassed the end

  of project targets as measured by restructured targets: PDO 1 on average number of students and researchers per laboratory workstations; PDO 2

  on number of internationally accredited programs and PDO 3 on number of proposed actions for autonomy implemented. Similarly, seven out of ten

  intermediate indicators have achieved/exceeded their targets, including for instance the number of scientific publications by researchers, lecturers

  and students from Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST) and Vietnam National University for Agriculture (VNUA) or the number of

  academic programs receiving an international accreditation in these two universities. The Ministry of Education and Training (MOET) completed and

  put in operation a new higher education management information system and a data base management system at MOET to be used by the Centers

  for Education Accreditation to monitor both academic programs' revision and certification as well the certification of higher education
