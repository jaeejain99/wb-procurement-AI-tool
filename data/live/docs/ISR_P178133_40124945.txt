4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The Project Development Objective is to provide consumption support to vulnerable households with pregnant women and mothers of children under 4 years of age and increase knowledge of nurturing care services that contribute to early childhood development (ECD). The Project provides financial resources and capacity support to (i) scale up beneficiary coverage of the Mother and Child Benefit Programme (MCBP) and (ii) strengthen MCBP institutional and monitoring systems and capacity.
In FY2026, MCBP enrolled 2,037,817 beneficiaries and also rolled out system interoperability with the District Health
Information System 2 (DHIS 2), allowing for monitoring of service utilization by MCBP beneficiaries. These reflect partial achievement of Performance Based Condition targets set under the Project.
The Mid-Term Review, which concluded in July 2026, finds that the Project remains highly relevant to Bangladesh’s development agenda. Early childhood development is the foundation of human capital and of a skilled, productive workforce. Intervening at the earliest stages of life offers a window of opportunity that enables future skills: nurturing care from pregnancy lays the cognitive and socio-emotional foundations for school readiness and offers the highest returns on investment.
Going forward, a Project restructuring is recommended to revise PBC targets that reflect evolving priorities and critical actions, including building on gains in urban coverage and interoperability of systems; aligning with country priorities, and prioritizing systems building, specifically on the MIS and associated institutional capacity.
