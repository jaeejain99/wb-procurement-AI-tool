1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 22-Sep-2023)
   The Program Development Objective (PDO) is to improve student learning and retention, and build system resilience in
   secondary education.

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The IDA Board of Executive Directors approved the Program on September 22, 2023. On October 31, 2023, the Executive
   Committee of National Economic Council, approved the Learning Acceleration in Secondary Education Program. The legal
   documents were signed by the Country Director of the World Bank and the Senior Secretary of the Economic Relations
   Division of the Government on November 22, 2023. The Bank has declared Program effectiveness on November 30, 2023.
   The project has successfully achieved all Year 1 Disbursement Linked Results and has completed necessary
   disbursements.
   The total disbursement remains US$135.9 million, representing 45% of the total credit, underscoring the effective pace of
   implementation and results delivery. Directorate of Secondary and Higher Education has submitted the Interim
   Unaudited Financial Report for the quarter ending June 30, 2025, and has received clearance from the Bank team on
   October 22, 2025
