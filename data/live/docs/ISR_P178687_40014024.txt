  Comments
  M&E rating is Satisfactory due to progress made by the Government of Nepal in tracking the DPC series’ results, including
  the greenhouse gas emission reduction and increased revenues from the new green tax. The Program Management
  rating is informed by the Ministry of Finance’s continued strong performance in coordinating the DPC line ministries and
  deliver the reform program convincingly across four governments since the start of the DPC series.



3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The second operation in the Nepal Programmatic Green, Resilient, Inclusive Development Policy Credit series of three
   operations was fully disbursed on April 25, 2025. The second operation was approved on December 12, 2024 and

  declared legally effective on March 17, 2025. The programmatic series’ objective for 2022-2026 is to improve the
  enabling environment for Nepal's green, resilient, and inclusive development pathway.

   Six policy actions have been successfully developed and approved by the Government of Nepal under the second
  operation, and are now being implemented by the Government. These six policy actions are part of the Government’s
  broader programmatic policy program, coordinated by the Ministry of Finance, which includes ten policy engagements
  that together aim to build resilience, and boost inclusive economic development and sustainability that helps create job
  opportunities. These policy engagements address clean air and industrial/vehicular emissions control, land use and
  administration, resilient infrastructure to withstand natural disaster and environmental risks, strategic investment
  planning, conservation financing and jobs from private sector development, forest sector development for jobs from
  production and protection as well as female entrepreneurship, climate-smart agriculture, inlcuding livestock health and
  irrigation, sustainable water resource management and water supply, shock-responsive social protection, and solid waste
  and plastic management.

  There have been no policy reversals across four consecutive Government administrations, and good progress toward a
  future DPC-3, showing how committed the Government is to its policy program. Several policy actions anticipated for
  the third operation in the series have already been completed or are well progressed, including a new water supply and
  sanitation regulation, approved on January 1, 2025, and a new standard for shock-responsive social protection, approved
  on December 31, 2024. In addition, progress toward the programmatic series results indicators is good but the full policy
  program will be needed to deliver the results. Relevant ministries have been collaborating effectively to advance new
  relevant policy actions and deliver the end of program results planned for fiscal year 2027.
