 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The project was closed with shortfall in the PDO level indicators (a) NSPSOs invite bids in National Competitive Bidding (NCB) through the e-GP

  system (b) SPSOs’ conduct contract management using e-GP (e-CMS) system (Percentage) and (c) Invite International Competitive Bidding

  (ICB) through the e-GP system (Number)

  The project is not likely to meet the following intermediatory indicators at project closing: (a) Government approved organogram with staffing of the

  proposed Authority; (b) Authority's officials in place compared with the staffing plan; (c) Strengthening the country’s emergency procurement and

  contract management framework; and (d) Disposal policy drafted and issued.
