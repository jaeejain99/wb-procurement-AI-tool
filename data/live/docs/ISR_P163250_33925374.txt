 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Overall project implementation is supervised by a Project Management Unit (PMU) within PKSF. As a result, progress towards achieving the Project

  Development Objective (PDO) “to increase the adoption of environmentally sustainable practices by targeted microenterprises” is on track.

  As of the mid of September 2022, SEP had introduced environmentally sustainable practices in 30 priority sub-sectors/clusters, 19 of which were

  also provided with common services that target MEs’ productivity. Beyond the cumulative target of 30,000 MEs to reach the 4th Year, 54,472 MEs

  (among which 84% are female) had signed loan agreements with POs under the project.

  Additionally, to enhance environmental knowledge and green business practices, the World Bank and PKSF jointly arranged a five-day technical

  virtual knowledge workshop from March 26 to April 6, 2022.

  Regarding the overall project’s progress, it was agreed to satisfy the request of PKSF to extend the project closing date to February 28, 2024, by

  eight months to complete the activities to meet the PDO. However, the project is experiencing a delay in implementation due to COVID-related

  obstruction with operations and procurement contracts.
