 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  GESTIP aimed to strengthen Ghana's energy sector, implement reforms, and improve planning and coordination. By the expected closing date of

  Dec 31, 2023, GESTIP had successfully achieved the primary objective of allocating power sector revenue through a Cash Waterfall Mechanism

  and developed and adopted the electrification strategy and investment plan and the Clean Cooking Strategy.
