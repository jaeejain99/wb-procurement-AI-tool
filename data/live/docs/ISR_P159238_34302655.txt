 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The project's overall implementation has been improved with completion of road sections under contracts CW 3, 4B, 5, 6 and 7 that are expected to

  be handed over and opened officially to traffic soon. However, implementation of contract CW1 is still behind schedule due to long-pending issues

  on land acquisition and resettlement in Tay Son district, Binh Dinh province. Ministry of Transport and PMU2 are continuing working with the

  province and district to resolve these pending issues to ensure completion of the project before the closing date.
