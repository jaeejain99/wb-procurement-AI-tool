 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The satisfactory momentum of the Project for achieving all six Project Development Objectives (PDO) has been observed and CPTU/IMED made a

  continuous effort to attain the high impactful milestones under different components. The project has progressed so far to achieve all six Project

  Development Objective (PDO) indicators - (i) reduction of procurement lead time for NCB using electronic government procurement (e-GP) has

  already exceeded the project end target – it takes an average of 57 days to complete procurement (invitation to contract award) against the end

  target of 70 days; (ii) newly selected 28 Public Sector Organization [27 NSPSOs + 1 NSPSO (LGIs) consist of 893 Public Sector Organizations

  covering municipalities, zilla parishads (district councils), upazila parishads (sub-district councils), and city corporations but excluding two large city

  corporations of Dhaka under the Local Government Division)] invited over 90% of their NCB procurement through e-GP against the PDO’s current

  target of 90% and project end target of 100%; (iii) citizen monitoring in contract implementation is ongoing in 48 upazilas (sub-districts) under 16

  districts of 8 divisions are in place, against the target of 45; (iv) Since the last mission the most notable progress has been achieved by completing
