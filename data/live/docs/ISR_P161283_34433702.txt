4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS





4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project progress is on track and all the major activities will be completed by the project closing. Almost all project

  development indicators and intermediate results are likely to achieve the targets by the project closing date except two

  PDO indicators on fully vaccination coverage for children under one year and service utilization at commune health

  stations.

  The project will be closed on time on December 31, 2024. The Ministry of Health has submitted the draft end project

  evaluation report to the World Bank in early December 2024. The World Bank is undertaking a final mission to prepare

  for a project’s Implementation Completion Results Report during December 2-13, 2024 aiming to finalize by April 2025.
