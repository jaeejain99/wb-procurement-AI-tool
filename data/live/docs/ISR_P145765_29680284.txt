           The World Bank                                                                          Implementation Status & Results Report

           Ghana Climate Innovation Center (P145765)







 Project implementation is progressing well under Component 1. Since the last mission, the GCIC’s first cohort of eleven (11) companies have completed

 the pre-incubation phase of their training, with six (6) companies receiving proof of concept grants totaling US$124,900. One cohort 1 business was

 named Ghana's "Innovative Energy Company of 2017" in a sign of the cohort's achievements. All 11 cohort 1 companies will continue to receive support

 from the GCIC for the next six months in the areas of venture acceleration, market access, access to finance and policy advocacy, as well as

 technology and product development. Selection has also finished for the second cohort of the GCIC, with an intake of twelve (12) companies, expected

 to be even stronger businesses than cohort 1. The GCIC leadership has also held its second advisory board meeting, chaired by the Deputy Minister of

 Environment, Science, Technology and Innovation and including donors and private sector leaders.





 Implementation of Component 2, the Ghana Climate Venture Facility, has not yet begun. The Bank, in agreement with Ashesi University and the

 government, competitively selected a new implementation partner for Component 2 to allow Ashesi to focus solely on implementation of the GCIC. This

 change necessitates a project restructuring that has now placed Component 2 a full 9 months behind its original implementation schedule. The World

 Bank is now engaging the government to receive consent for the new Component 2 implementation arrangements, so implementation can get

 underway.





 Project development objectives are still achievable within the project timeline if implementation of Component 1 going forward maintains the efficiency

 and effectiveness shown during 2017. Delays in receiving the government consent for Component 2 implementation arrangements raises the risk that

 some project targets related to improving access to early stage financing for climate technology companies will not be achieved. The World Bank project

 task team will continue to work with Government to closely monitor implementation progress to ensure overall progress and efficient implementation is

 maintained throughout the project implementation period.
