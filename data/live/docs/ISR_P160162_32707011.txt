 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The project has made significant progress in the rehabilitation and construction of urban infrastructure, such as out of 17 contracts, 9 contracts with

  a value of USD24.185 million have been signed. Once land acquisition is completed, two sections of Huong Thuong-Chua Hang Road with a total of

  approximately USD5.62 million will commence their construction.

  Technical Assistance activities are well underway with consultancies for integrated planning, public transport planning, and urban infrastructure

  asset management plans about to be launched with the aim of improving the City’s capacities for integrated urban planning and management.
