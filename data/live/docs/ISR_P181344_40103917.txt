3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
During the reporting period, the WB team noted that the GON through the respective ministries and departments are
making progress towards the achievement of the result indicators (RI) and intermediate result indicators (IRI) under the
Cat DDO.

PA 1 (RI#1 and IRI#1.1): The National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority (NDRRMA) confirmed the
transfer of resources to local governments from the Central Disaster Management Fund (DMF) supported by evidencedbased systems for damage assessment, financial management and construction monitoring.
PA 2 (RI#2 and IRI#2.1): Progress in developing a centralized electronic building permit system (e-BPS) has been slow. The
Department of Urban Development and Building Control (DUDBC) is in the process of hiring a firm to develop the
centralized E-BPS. Futhermore, municipalities using stand-alone e-BPS now stand at 42, moving up from 21 in September
2024.
PA 3 (RI#3 and IRI#3.1): The Department of Hydrology and Meteorology (DHM) expanded its early warning system,
providing hazard warnings for heavy rain and floods to over 7.4 million people in 2025 through partnerships with telecom
companies.
PA 4 (RI#4 and RI#5): The Ministry of Energy, Water Resources, and Irrigation (MoEWRI) successfully developed River
Management Plans for the Balen and Daraudi rivers and completed 55 km of riverbank protection works through its
2025/2026 budget.
PA 5 (RI#6): The Ministry of Health and Population (MoHP) is implementing a surveillance system to monitor all 52
priority diseases and has updated the Early. Warning and Reporting System (EWARS) Standard Operating Procedure
(SOP), mandating daily mandatory reporting of 8 syndromes and 18 diseases.
