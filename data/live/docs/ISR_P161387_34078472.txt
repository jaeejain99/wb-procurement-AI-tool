 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The operation achieved a significant number of the program results during this reporting period. Based on the preliminary Independent Verification

  Agent (IVA) report (March 2023), additional 18 DLRs amounting to US$ 76.5 million and €9.7 million under the World Bank and AFD respectively

  were achieved. This brings the total number of achieved DLRs to 47 (out of 50), representing 95% of the overall World Bank and AFD financing.

  The related program Disbursement Linked Indicators (DLIs) noted to be fully achieved during this reporting period include: (i) DLI 4 - consolidation

  of staff data for better human resource management; (ii) DLI 5 - timeliness and quality of financial statements preparation and audits; (iii) DLI 6-

  strengthened fiduciary assurance and transparency; (iv) DLI 7 - transparency and oversight of State Corporations - development of the GIMIS; (v)

  DLI 8 - strengthened service delivery through e-government services automation (e-citizen).

  The three pending DLRs on e-government procurement, public investment management, and cash management have shown substantial

  implementation progress. They are on track to be met and fully disbursed by the program closing date on December 31, 2023.
