1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 02-Jun-2022)
   The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to strengthen sector institutional capacity for service delivery under
   federalism and increase access to improved water supply and sanitation services in the participating municipalities.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   Yes


   Current Development Objective (Approved as part of Restructuring package Seq. 2 on 01-Sep-2025)
   The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to strengthen the sector's institutional capacity for service delivery under
   federalism and to provide safely managed water supply services in participating municipalities.

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Progress towards achievement of the PDO and Implementation Progress (IP) ratings remain Moderately Satisfactory.
  The Project has made notable progress in the award of water supply scheme contracts, and steady implementation
  progress has been observed across major contracts, with a positive implementation trajectory anticipated to continue.
  However, risks of implementation delays remain. The Project is also making progress toward the operationalization of the
  professionalized water supply and sanitation service provider and the regulatory unit within DWSSM.
