 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The Project Development Objective is to improve access to infrastructure in priority areas and improve urban planning in the participating cities.

  Implementation has made good progress in the past months, and presently, 341,428 people are provided with improved living conditions and

  62,319 people have access to improved basic urban infrastructure facilities and services thanks to investments in tertiary infrastructure upgrading in

  low income areas and priority and secondary infrastructure in the targeted cities of Bac Lieu, Ben Tre, Long Xuyen, Soc Trang, Tan An, Vi Thanh,

  and Vinh Long. This includes 32.9 km of new and improved lanes and 11.7 km of rehabilitated drainage canals in low income areas; 11,974

  households with new or improved connection to sewer lines; 30.1 km of constructed/improved secondary road; and there are 30.6 km of
