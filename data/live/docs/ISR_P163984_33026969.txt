 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  GESTIP has made clear progress in advancing project activities. Since the August 2020 MTR, the PIU has doubled the number of ongoing

  (contracted) activities. However, delays remain largely due to the time taken for preparation of the ToRs followed by time for proposal evaluation,

  presenting a risk to the achievement of the development outcome.



  Ongoing and future (to be contracted) GESTIP activities are aimed at strengthening the capacity of the energy sector to implement sector reforms

  and improve energy sector planning and coordination in Ghana; the activities are essential to the implementation of the Energy Sector Recovery
