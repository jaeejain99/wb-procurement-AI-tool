 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Notable progress has been registered in the achievement of results since the last program supervision mission that took place in January 2020.

  Total disbursements linked to the achievement of Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs) was US$37.07 million in January 2020. As of August 2020,

  13 DLIs (out of a total of 27) have been achieved, prompting the Bank's disbursement of the USD 21.5 million associated with these DLIs. These

  achievements have been registered in the following areas: a) improved prioritization and management of public investments, b) improved borrowing

  and cash management practices to enable reliable funding for service delivery and investment projects, c) consolidation of staff data for better

  human resource management, d) the timeliness and quality of financial statements, and e) the quality of external and internal audit

  practices (specifically DLR 1a, DLR 1b, DLR 2.1a, DLR 2.1d, DLR 2.3a, DLR 4a, DLR 5.1, DLR 5.2a, DLR 5.2b, DLR 5.2c, DLR 5.2d, DLR 6.2a,

  and DLR 6.2b).



  The Government has established a Public Investment Management (PIM) Unit and introduced a PIM Manual to guide the decisions regarding the

  identification, design, and implementation of public investment projects based on internationally recognized good practices on the design, appraisal,

  and monitoring of capital projects. The National Treasury has also completed the preparation of an aggregate cash plan and the design of an

  automated cash management system to improve the predictability of fund flows regarding government services and projects. There also has been

  progress regarding the preparation of financial statements that are consistent with the expenditure information contained in the Integrated Financial

  Management Information System (IFMIS), as well as in internal audit, with the completion of an Internal Audit Manual that lays out the guidelines for

  internal audit in government agencies in line with international good practice.



  The Government provided evidence for the achievement of these DLIs to an Independent Verification Agent (IVA) engaged through the project to

  verify achievements prior to the disbursement of funds. Upon receiving and reviewing the findings of the verification process, the Bank endorsed the

  IVA's conclusion that the prerequisite conditions have been met for the release of the funds and is in the process of making the funds available for

  withdrawal.



  The progress achieved under this program is anchored in the Government’s continuing commitment to public financial management (PFM) reforms

  in the context of its PFM Reform Strategy. The GESDeK Program continues to benefit from the strong institutional foundations for PFM reforms that

  the Government has been building over the last several years, including recent initiatives by the PFM Reform Secretariat to further

  strengthen project implementation arrangements by instituting Focal Persons and Results Teams with a Result Area Lead who are designated with

  the responsibility to deliver the relevant DLIs under each Result Area.



  The Medium-Term Review (MTR) for the program is tentatively scheduled for November 30 - December 4, 2020. The MTR will provide a platform

  to review the efficacy and relevance of the program in the context of the evolving policy environment and emerging development priorities and

  restructure the program as necessary to ensure that it continues to effectively support the implementation of the PFM Reform Strategy.
