            Ghana Financial Sector Development Project (P161787)







  The restructuring of the Project was completed on June 7, 2027. Among other changes, the restructuring cancelled some activities to reflect

  changes in priorities and modified the Project Development Objective (PDO) to better align it with the remaining project activities. The revised PDO

  is to increase access to financial services by individuals. The Project closing date was extended to June 30, 2024.
