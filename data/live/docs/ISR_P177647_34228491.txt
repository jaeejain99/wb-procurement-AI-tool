 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The School Sector Transformation Program (SSTP) Operation was approved on March 24, 2023 by the World Bank's Board of Executive Directors'.
  The Operation includes financing from International Development Association for an amount of US$120 million, and financing from Global
  Partnership for Education for an amount of US$19.7 million. The signing of the Financing Agreement and the Grant Agreement was completed on
  May 31, 2023. The SSTP, together with other Joint Financing Partners (JFPs) and non-JFP development partners supports the implementation of
  the School Education Sector Plan (SESP) 2022-3023. Overall, the program is making good progress towards achieving the program development
  objectives to improve literacy and numeracy as measured through the national assessment of reading and numeracy (NARN) and to increase the
  number of local-level governments that receive performance grants. For the assessment of the progress in terms of equitable access, the final Flash
  Report is expected to become available in January 2024 to provide the net enrollment rate for grades 9-12. In addition, most intermediate results
  indicators are on track, while a few await the final Flash Report for the latest data.
  The absence of the required legal framework to be provided by the Federal Education Act remains one of the main priorities in terms of enabling a
  full-fledged implementation of the SESP. An Education Bill was submitted to the Parliament in October 2023. The recent Joint Review Meeting for
  the SESP stressed the importance of its swift enactment to strengthen and institutionalize the coordination and collaboration across the different
  tiers of government in the school education sector. Capacity building of local-level governments and schools is critical to mitigate the institutional
  risks, accelerate the project implementation and improve the impacts on students at the school level.
