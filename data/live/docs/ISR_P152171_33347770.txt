 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Among the most significant achievements of the Ghana Economic Management Strengthening (GEMS) project is having successfully supported the

  establishment of the State Interests and Governance Authority (SIGA).

  The GEMS Project was restructured including a no cost extension of its closing date from March 31, 2020 to March 31, 2022. This was to ensure

  that all critical activities and procurement packages across project components are finalized in order to achieve the Project Development Objective

  (PDO).

  Component 1: Strengthening Ghana Revenue Authority’s Business Intelligence Systems

  The objective of the component is to improve tax compliance, integrate income assessment regardless of source and location, simplify the

  processes, and align with international tax rules. This objective is expected to be achieved through the implementation of the Data Warehouse

  deployment and integration of the Common Reporting Standards (CRS)/Foreign Account Tax Compliance Account (FATCA) platforms with the DW.

  The procurement process on contracting the BI/DW has been delayed, but expected to be completed before project closing.

  Component 2: Strengthening Debt Management

  Component 2 aims to strengthen the Ministry of Finance’s (MoF) capacity to manage public debt with a high degree of transparency, as well as to

  improve treasury management and forecasting.

  Progress has been made under Component 2, such as successfully strengthening capacity, developing and implementing Ghana’s Medium-Term

  Debt Management Strategy (MTDS). The remaining challenge under the component is to finalize the Business Continuity Plan (BCP) to improve

  operational risk management.

  Component 3: Strengthening Capacity in Public Investment Management (PIM)

  The objective of Component 3 is to develop and institutionalize a functional, transparent process for public investment programming that is applied

  across the Government. It seeks to achieve the objective through the establishment and implementation of a functional PIM system; and enhancing

  the capacity of the National Development Planning Commission (NDPC) for long-term planning of infrastructure and other investment projects.

  Considerable progress has been made in developing the necessary manuals, guidelines, Human Resource (HR), and long-term institutional training

  capacity. However, the procurement of ICT hardware and software relevant to PIM which was originally a planned under Component 3, has not

  been implemented. Instead, an Integrated Bank of Projects (IBP), to be fully funded by the government, is planned to be placed under the Public

  Investment Program (PIP) Unit of the MoF and designed to maintain a comprehensive and reliable electronic database of public projects. However,

  the procurement of the IBP has been delayed due to the steps required by Parliament.

  Component 4: Improving the Governance of State-Owned Enterprises (SOE)

  The objective of this component is to improve SOE governance by supporting reforms aimed at consolidating the state’s ownership role through an

  equity study and preparatory work for setting up a Single Entity responsible for overseeing SOEs; establishing the SOE Single Entity; and piloting

  corporate governance improvements in five SOEs.

  Component 4 supported the major achievement of the establishment of SIGA and the progress has been positive, but PDO Indicator 4 has not been

  fully met. Publication of State Ownership Reports 2019 and 2020 has been, partly due to the COVID-19 pandemic, delayed.
