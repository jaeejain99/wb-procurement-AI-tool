3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

The program is in its fourth year of implementation. Key activities are progressing, and achievements
supported through the operation include the following:
1. Foundational Learning Roadmap. The Ministry has developed a draft foundational learning
roadmap, an important initiative to address the critical issue of low foundational learning levels in
Nepal. The roadmap should move beyond a strategic narrative and include a clear implementation
plan with prioritized actions, timelines, outputs, and monitoring indicators.
2. Implementation and evaluation of the Recovery and Accelerated Learning (ReAL) plan. The
ReAL plan has been implemented nationwide, with intensive support provided in 250 local
governments. An external evaluation of ReAL’s effectiveness has also been completed. Based on

lessons and experience from implementing the ReAL plan, CEHRD continues to implement
remedial learning activities targeting students with low learning levels.
3. Support for implementation of the Integrated Curriculum (IC). Over the past four years, nearly
600 local governments have received additional support to strengthen IC implementation in the
early grades. This support included the provision of additional learning materials for students,
teacher guides, and customized teacher training. At least one teacher from each school in these
600 local governments received customized IC training. Given the persistent low learning levels, an
additional DLI target has been added through restructuring for the final year of the operation to
provide IC training to all remaining early grade teachers in the four provinces with the lowest
learning outcomes in NARN 2024.
4. Citizen Engagement. The 2026 BRM invited representatives from local and provincial
governments, teacher associations, School Management Committee associations, and civil society
organizations (CSOs), including the National Campaign for Education in Nepal (NCE-N) and the
Association of INGOs in Nepal–Education Working Group (AIN-EWG). These stakeholders shared
best practices, contributions, challenges, and constraints related to accessing quality education, as
well as potential ways forward. The biannual review meetings provide a platform for broader
stakeholder engagement and an opportunity to reinforce the importance of ongoing dialogue
between the government and stakeholders, while also enabling reflection on how CSOs can
contribute to achieving the targets and commitments outlined in the SESP.
