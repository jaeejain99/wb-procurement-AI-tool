 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Summary of Project Implementation Status and Progress
  Component A. Improving the Coordination and Management of Statistical Activities
     1. Mechanisms to enhance coordination: After the formation and approval of the National Advisory Council on Statistics (NACS) by the
        Honorable Prime Minister, which was gazette on 12 January 2021, the Council held its second annual meeting on April 6, 2022, following
        the first meeting held June 27, 2021, with support from the project. The Project Appraisal Document (PAD) specifies the formation of the
        National Statistics Coordination Council (NSCC), but the mission was informed of decision made in 2020 by the BBS and SID
        management to change the naming of the committee to National Advisory Council on Statistics (NACS). Just as described for NSCC in the
        PAD, the NACS Committee comprise of high-level members from different government agencies producing statistics and coordinated by

             BBS. The NACS’s aim is to support the National Statistical Systems (NSS)’s vision in Bangladesh of becoming a world-class provider of
             authentic, relevant, and timely data in a holistic and user-friendly manner. The NACS was to meet at least once a year to provide overall
             guidance and coordination to the National Statistical System (NSS) and having so far held at least two annual meetings the project has
             achieved its end target objective.
        2. Memorandum of Understanding (MoU): The mission was informed that the reports on existing MoUs and the use of administrative data
             for official statistics were revised and updated with some suggestions for further improvement. Around 6 Government Ministries, Divisions,
             Departments and Agencies were finally identified for signing of MoUs. These include: (1) Bangladesh Bank, (2) Financial Institutions
             Division (FID), (3) Department of Agriculture Extension (DAE), (4) Ministry of Industry, (5) National Institute of Population Research and
             Training (NIPORT), and (6) Bangladesh Bureau of Educational Information and Statistics (BANBEIS). Towards this effort two meetings
             were held. The first meeting to discuss modalities for signing of the MoUs was held on March 9, 2022, in presence of the 6 Government
             Ministries, Divisions, Departments and Agencies which also coincided with the project supervision mission. Further, a follow-up workshop
             was held on March 14, 2022, to discuss filling in the questionnaire on Administrative Register. During the workshop, good practices in
             establishing Memorandums of Understanding for data sharing were discussed as part of the technical assistance.
        3. Reporting on current practices in HR Recruitment and Manual for HR Management in BBS. The mission was briefed that a report on
             current practices in HR recruitment has been produced. The report aims at providing guidelines for improving the recruitment process of
             staff at the BBS, and in particular the temporary staff, including survey enumerators. The focus on this group of employees has been
             decided during the inception mission of the Key Expert on national statistical coordination (January 2021), in view of the important
             operations that the BBS is planning (Population Census, Household Income and Expenditure Survey, and LFS etc.). The report describes
             the enumerator job description following the Generic Statistical Business Process Model (GSPBM), which is an international standard, that
             determine the number, competences and skills required of enumerators and presents the manual of HR management. Attracting and
             retaining people is a key success factor. Attracting and retaining the best candidates is a major objective of Human Resources
             Management and Training (HRMT). To achieve this “an attractive package” needs to be created, including positive workplace atmosphere,
             flexible working time, quality training, diversity, etc. BBS needs to commit fully to make this happen – top management support, open
             communication and incorporation in policies and appraisal systems.
  Component B. Developing Human Resources and ICT Infrastructure to Produce and Manage Dat
    4. Investing in core skills and competencies: This component partly focuses on the development of Human Resources (HR) and includes the
  drafting of a training policy as well as the full “training engineering cycle” that starts from the identification of training needs and supply, the design,
  and the implementation of a training program. The objective was to develop human resources by investing in core skills and competencies. The
  mission was brought to speed on the trainings conducted since the September 2021 mission based on the Training needs assessment and
  inventory of statistical trainings. From the last Implementation Support Mission (ISM) in September 2021, the following trainings were however
  conducted:
            The training on Fundamentals of price statistics on 23-24 of October 2021, whose participants included specialists from Price Statistics
             and National Accounting Wings. The 2-day training explained the collection, processing, analysis, and dissemination of Price Statistics
             Wing.
            Training course on Sampling and sampling techniques. This training took place at the Rural Development Academy (RDA), Bogra,
             during 4-14 of October 2021. The course aimed at increasing the capacity of BBS to implement household, business, and price surveys,
             by training 35 specialists.
            A 2-day training on the Fundamentals of Labor Statistics and QLFS questionnaire held on 24-25 of November 2021. The principal
             objective of the training was to enhance the capacity of participants to contribute to the meaningful development of the Labor Market
             Information System in Bangladesh, in particular the collection of appropriate and accurate data in line with international standards and the
             interpretation of statistics obtained considering national policies and circumstances.
            First training / study visit on the Population and Housing Census conducted by Spain (INE Spain) and Turkey (SESRIC) for five senior
             delegates from the BBS from 29 October – 9 November 2021.
  5. Investments in ICT infrastructure to support data collection and management: The mission was informed that the key experts from the
  Twinning Partner (TP) with support from BBS Computer Wing staff and the NSDS Project IT Consultant, has prepared the draft comprehensive ICT
  Plan and submitted to the BBS for review and TP to update it further after receiving input and comments. This is based on initial consultative
  meetings held between the TP and BBS Computer Wing. The plan covers the entire statistical process i.e., from data collection to data
  dissemination with the goal of building an integrated system for the production and dissemination of statistics. Already, all the ongoing and
  upcoming surveys, including the 2022 HIES, 2022 QLFS, and the 2022 Population and Housing Census are utilizing digital technologies in data
  collection, mainly the Computer Assisted Personal Interviewing (CAPI) method using digital device. The project has procured several ICT
  equipment, including computers, printers, and other equipment for office space. Given the delay in preparation of the ICT Plan all the currently
  procured ICT equipment are based on the immediate need basis and as per requirements of the various wings of BBS.
  6. Developing the website: To improve accessibility and dissemination of statistical information the TP has prepared a prototype website for BBS
  and a “website validation development” group was created on 12/12/2021 to review and validate. The group is composed of representative from the
  different BBS wings, Twinning Partner experts and the PIU staff, and is expected to meet monthly to advance the website development and validate
  the different milestones of the construction of the website and its sustainability. The PIU is also to set up a final date for the presentation of the
  prototype website to the validation group to consider and approve the new design, data, and information for upload, agree on the server to host it
  during the development and transfer to BBS, and for BBS and SID management approval. Currently, dissemination through websites exists but
  could be improved in terms of ease of navigation, provision and easier access to statistical information and related metadata and improved
  communication towards end users. Some publications are currently made available on the website (bbs.gov.bd) but the way they are presented to
  end user could be enhanced to facilitate improved accessibility and use. Appropriate reference metadata are often missing. Some data are made
  available on the open data government portal (http://data.gov.bd/) where some data can be downloaded in their storage format which can be csv,
  pdf, dbf, Stata depending on what is available. Some census and surveys aggregated tables can also be dynamically selected on a subsite
  (http://redatam.bbs.gov.bd/) and presented in the format of single or crossed tables. Microdata seems to be not available. Nevertheless, a web site

  Component C. Improving the Coverage and Quality of Core Statistics Required for Policy Making
  7. Household Income and Expenditure Survey (2022) and Quarterly Labor Force Survey: After a prolonged delay both the Household Income
  and Expenditure Survey (HIES 2022) and the Quarterly Labor Force Survey (LFS, 2022) are currently under implementation. Both surveys were
  started on January 1, 2022. While HIES will be implemented between January – December 2022, LFS will be a quarterly survey to be implemented
  every 3 months potentially for the next 3 years, from January 2022 to December 2024. For the first time both surveys are implemented using
  Computer Assisted Personal Interviews (CAPI) and data is transmitted in real-time for quality checks and monitoring. Both surveys also use
  mapping and household listing information available from the preparatory phase of the 2022 Housing and Population Census (HPC) for the
  sampling frame. The mission learnt that through the support of NSDS project the HIES project team were able to undertake Training of Trainers,
  conduct residential training for enumerators and data entry operators (in December 2021) and testing of the CAPI questionnaire and refresher
  trainings for both the HIES and LFS enumerators.
  8. Improving the Coverage and Quality of Core Statistics: From the last mission in September 2021 several activities were completed. In
  summary these include preparation of training materials and delivery of various trainings, review of the quality and coverage of current social
  statistics produced and disseminated by BBS and review of current sampling design of the latest household surveys and recommendations on
  improvements. Other activities completed included; analysis of statistics based on administrative records for demographic, health and gender
  statistics, and cross-check the quality of such data with household-based statistics, designed metadata template for social statistics and preparation
  of a new method for processing foreign merchandise trade data.
  Component D. Promoting and Strengthening Access to and the Use of Official Statistics
  9. Documenting statistical activities and providing better access to data: To support the key project goal of documentation, microdata
  anonymization and making data accessible for further research and analysis, the mission was informed that several activities were completed or
  initiated from the previous ISM period in September 2021. These include the first draft deliverables on survey documentation, data visualization,
  preparation of codes and syntax and micro data anonymization. On data visualization a report was prepared with brief descriptions of selected
  terms, types of users, types of products, metadata, and user stories. Surveys that were shared in different forms cover Health Statistics (MICS-
  2019, HMS-2014), Labor Statistics (LFS 2016-17), Industry Statistics (SMI-2019) and Poverty/Welfare Statistics (HIES-2016).
  Component E. Project Management
  10. Revision and approval of the Technical Assistance Project Proposal (TAPP) and the Twinning Partner (TP) Contract: After the project
  closing date extension to June 30, 2023, two of the key pending tasks were the revision and approval of TAPP and the adjustment and signing of
  the TP contract to allow for the completion of activities and reflect changes and recommendation of the project Mid-Term Review of March 2021.
  The mission confirmed the progress made to extend the TAPP and the TP contract which has now been concluded with the approval of TAPP and
  signing of the TP contract. However, the extension date for both documents were made to February 28, 2022 - a different date from that of the
  project closing date of June 30, 2023.
  11. Twinning Partnership (TP). The progress in implementation of the TP contract is satisfactory having successfully completed more than half of
  the deliverables. The mission learnt that the TP has delivered 17 final and draft deliverables out of the 24 total project deliverables across the four
  main project components (Component A, B, C and D) and curried out 7 local basic trainings as well as subject matter and advance trainings.
  Further, the TP has prepared core and subject matter questionnaires, organized virtual meetings with BBS and other partners, and prepared project
  visibility website NSDS for Bangladesh – BBS, Bangladesh (devstat.com). In addition, the TP held 3 key experts’ missions to Dhaka, Bangladesh
  one of which coincided with the ISM which was useful to the mission team. Joint meetings were therefore organized during the mission with other
  data producers to initiate protocols and discuss progress on the MoUs.
