1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 19-Dec-2024)
   To improve access and utilization of quality health and nutrition services and build health system resilience in select
   geographical areas.

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The Program-for-Results (PforR) mobilizes support to the government of Bangladesh fifth health, nutrition and population
   sector program. The sector program is in the final stages of approval and once done, the legal agreements for the PforR
   will be signed between the government and the World Bank and implementation can begin.
