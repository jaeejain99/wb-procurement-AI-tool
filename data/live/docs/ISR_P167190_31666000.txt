 Project Development Objectives
  Program Development Objective (from Program Document)
  The Bangladesh Jobs DPC supports the Government of Bangladesh's program of reforms to address the country’s jobs challenges by: (i)
  modernizing the trade and investment environment; (ii) strengthening systems that protect workers and build resilience; and (iii) improving policies
  and programs that enhance access to jobs for vulnerable populations.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No
