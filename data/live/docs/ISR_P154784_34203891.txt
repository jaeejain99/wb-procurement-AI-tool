 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  This ISR is based on the implementation support mission undertaken from November 7 to 10, 2023. The project was approved by the board in Feb

  2017 and became effective in May 2017. The project is scheduled to close on November 30, 2023. The project has been consistently performing

  well over the last several missions and has achieved 100 % disbursement. The project has achieved all PDO indicators and almost all of the

  intermediate indicators.

  The component wise achievements under the project are given below

  Component 1: Upscaling Climate-Smart Agricultural Practices

           Nearly 23,525 Common Interest Groups (CIGs) and Vulnerable and Marginalized Groups (VMGs) covering 536,000 farmers have been

            mobilized thus far, of which nearly 55 % are women farmers. These have been federated into 144 Community Driven Development

            Committees (CDDCs – One in every project ward) and 133 Savings and Credit Cooperatives (SACCOs) that have been established and

            registered with dedicated bank accounts.

           11,358 Micro-projects have been disbursed thus far, surpassing the project target.

           More than 492,170 farmers have reported to have adopted at least 1 Technology, Innovation and Management Practices (TIMPs)

           20,580 lead farmers have been identified at CIG level and trained to support community-based extension along with 4,384 Farmer Field

            Schools operationalized thus far.

           A total of 190 sub projects have been taken up for implementation under the project. Of these, 187 sub projects have already completed

            and handed over to the beneficiaries with significant impetus on community level capacity building for sustainability. The remaining 3 sub

            projects are expected to be completed by November 15, 2023.

           An additional 11,442 acres of land has been brought under irrigation because of the sub projects.

           199 Farmer Producer Organizations have been mobilized, with 48% of CIGs and VMGs linked to these FPOs. In addition to universal

            inclusion grants, 53 FPOs have received Enterprise Development Plan (EDP) grants to scale up value chain operations.

           More than 200,000 farmers have accessed digital agriculture technologies and over 147,000 of them report to have adopted new

            practices.

  Component 2: Strengthening Climate-Smart Agricultural Research & Seed Systems

           1594 Technologies Innovations and Management Practices (TIMPS) across 19 value chains are ready for upscaling and several of them

            are being disseminated to the 24 counties.

           20,580 lead farmers have been trained on the climate smart TIMPs by KALRO

           330 CIGs are undertaking commercial seed production. In addition, Kenya Agriculture Livestock Research Organization (KALRO) has

            cumulatively produced 4822 MT of climate smart early generation and certified seed (including KALRO’s seed unit) that could be

            accessed by the project beneficiaries.

           A total of 172 scholarships (97 MSc and 75 PhD) have been awarded under the component 2 and 3 jointly.

  Component 3: Supporting Agro-weather, Market, Climate and Advisory Services

           192 New and refurbished agro-, automatic weather stations (AWS) and hydro-meteorological facilities have been installed with the project

            support. These AWSs are supplying fortnightly data to the Big Data platform to enable agro weather advisories to be provided to farmers.

           315 Markets uploading market information on the Kenya Agricultural Market Information System (KAMIS). 70 Countries in Africa, Asia,

            Australia, Europe, Middle East, North & South America accessing KAMIS data and the Ministerial bi-weekly food security reports derives

            market data from KAMIS.

           Big Data platform and High Performing Computing System supplied, configured, installed, and commissioned at KALRO. A farmer

            database of 5.5 million farmers has been updated and all of these farmers are receiving integrated agro weather and market advisories.

           Three Data Services developed as part of the Big Data system: a) Crop/Livestock Selector b) Crop/ Livestock Suitability Mapping c)

            Crop/Livestock Modelling. Dissemination of integrated weather and market agro-advisories launched in all 45 rural Counties. The call

            centre/ interactive voice recording system (IVRS) has been commissioned.

           Supported the expansion of coverage for crop insurance (through the area yield index) to 39 counties. 1.2 million farmers have accessed

            crop insurance.

  Given the continued good progress since the last implementation support mission across all components , both the PDO and IP ratings are being

  retained as "Satisfactory".
