4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Implementation progress across the project has been slower than envisaged. Tendering for the Fulchari riverbank
  protection has started, to be followed by tendering for similar works at Kalihati by the end of October 2025. Timely award
  of contracts is needed so that works can start during the 2025 monsoon season. Under component 3, the areas to benefit
  from flood insurance has been selected. The insurance product and community protection fund are targeted to achieve
  operational readiness ahead of the 2026 monsoon season.
