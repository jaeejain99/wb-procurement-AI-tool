4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
Project implementation remains slow compared to the plan. Works are ongoing in six road upgradation contracts (70 km,
US$13 Million) in Madhesh, Sudurpaschim, and Karnali provinces. Bid docs for Kalaiya Malahi road has been finalized and bid notice will be published soon. Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) for an additional 18 roads are being finalized across the three provinces. For new connectivity, one contract for Kalikot Mahawai road (6 kms) has been signed, bid docs are being finalized for Baitadi Sigas road (30 km) and DPRs of two unconnected municipalities i.e., Bajhang-Saipal and Dolpa-
Chharkatansen are being finalized. Periodic maintenance covering 255 km is progressing, with contract signed for 17 out of planned 18 roads. Routine maintenance work is initiated in Madhesh and Sudurpaschim, by mobilizing community-
based road maintenance groups. The immediate priority going foward is to accelerate DPR finalization and initiate procurement of remaining roads.
