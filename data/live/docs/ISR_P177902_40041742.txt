  This is an interim ISR being submitted to update on critical milestones the Nepal: ACCESS Project (P177902) has been
  achieved. Following which the Implementation Progress (IP) rating will be upgraded from Moderately Unsatisfactory
  (MU) to Moderately Satisfactory (MS). Other Performance ratings and the Results Framework in this ISR remain
  unchanged from the last formal review conducted in March/April 2025. A full-fledged ISR will be submitted following the
  findings of the seventh implementation support review scheduled for early November 2025.
  ACCESS has reached the following milestone toward upgrading the IP rating from MU to MS: (i) project restructuring was
  concluded at the end of June 2025; and (ii) bid documents were submitted for two contracts — Gurusinghe-
  Chandrauta road (19 km) and Tinau Bridge works — as agreed by end-April 2025. Additionally, ongoing contracts under
  the Butwal Gorusinghe 50 km road sections are progressing well and the Project surpassed the disbursement target set
  for last Fiscal Year (FY). Going forwards, the Project is expected to continue accelerating implementation progress of the
  ongoing works, expedite procurement for remaining work packages, and strive to meet the disbursement targets set for
  this FY. In light of this progress and completion of proactivity measures, it is recommended that the IP rating of the
  Project be upgraded to MS.
