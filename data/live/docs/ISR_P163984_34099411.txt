 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  GESTIP activities are aimed at strengthening the capacity of the energy sector to implement sector reforms and improve energy sector planning

  and coordination in Ghana; the activities are essential to the implementation of the Energy Sector Recovery Program (ESRP), dedicated to

  improving sector finances. GESTIP became effective in February 2019 and was restructured in June 2022 to accommodate evolving sector

  priorities. The project close date is December 2023. GESTIP has made clear progress in advancing project activities. Three key results have been

  achieved so far and include: (a) 100 percent of power sector revenues are allocated to sector entities through the Cash Waterfall Mechanism; (b)

  Gas supply and demand study is now complete; (c) Utility Performance Improvement Plan Guidelines have been prepared, (d) NEDCO’s

  distribution system plan along with its specifications and construction standards updated, and (e) roughly 135 sector staff (of a targeted 50 and
