1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 31-Oct-2023)
   The Project Development Objective is to provide consumption support to vulnerable households with pregnant women
   and mothers of children under 4 years of age and increase knowledge of nurturing care services that contribute to early
   childhood development (ECD).

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  As of April 2026, 1,733,753 women are covered under the Mother and Child Benefit Programme (MCBP), comprising
  existing beneficiaries and newly enrolled beneficiaries.

  Progress towards achieving Performance Based Conditions (PBC): There are three PBC targets scheduled for FY2026,
  achievement of which would release up to US$35 million in early FY2027. Of the three targets, one is at high risk of not
  being achieved (PBC 3.2: Mother and Child Benefit Programme increases beneficiary coverage by at least 7 percent over
  previous FY), while the other two targets (PBC 2.2: Management Information System monitoring service utilization in at
  least 50 percent of locations and PBC 4.1: Annual Programme review conducted in at least 20 percent of locations and
  stakeholder consultations held to review findings and inform Programme updates) will not be met.

  Mid Term Review: The MTR is planned for July 2026 and will inform the scope of Project restructuring and TAPP revision.
  Anticipated changes include revision of PBC targets and allocations, prioritizing activities that are feasible to be
  completed in the remaining Project lifetime, updating the Results Framework, reviewing implementation arrangements,
  and potential partial cancellation.
