 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The project's progress toward achieving the project development objectives has been affected by the COVID-19 pandemic, especially in the last

  quarter of 2021 and particularly for construction and training activities. In the last six months, the project has achieved the following key results: (i)

  civil works for 48 commune health stations (CHS) and 5 district health centers (DHC) were completed and are operational in 6/13 provinces; (ii) civil

  works contracts for 154 CHSs and 5 DHCs have been awarded in 11 provinces; (iii) two packages of equipment were procured in two provinces; (iv)

  242 frontline health workers were trained for different health care services and techniques; (v) 3 training of trainer (TOT) training courses were

  delivered for balanced scorecard; (vi) a policy consultation workshop for grassroots health services was organized with the attendance of
