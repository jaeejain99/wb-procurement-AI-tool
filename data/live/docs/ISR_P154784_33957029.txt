            Kenya Climate Smart Agriculture Project (P154784)







  In terms of project results, there are several notable achievements both at the county level and the national level. A total of 713,000 farmers across

  24 counties are receiving a range of benefits from input support to access to modern agriculture technologies, innovations and management

  practices (TIMPs) to access to markets and access a range of agro-weather and market advisories. The sustainability of the project interventions is

  envisaged through the strengthening of the community institutions namely, 144 Community Driven Development Committees (CDDCs – One in

  every project ward) who will be transformed into Savings and Credit Cooperatives (SACCOs) and 199 Producer Organizations (POs- at least 5 in

  every project county) that have been established and registered with dedicated bank accounts. These CDDCs/SACCOs and POs will last beyond

  the project period and ensure that project farmers are able to continue accessing a range of input services, extension, and market linkages. In

  addition, a total of 191 sub project investments (water related investments, market infrastructure related investments) have been taken up for

  implementation under the project. Of these, 134 sub projects have already completed implementation and the rest 56 sub projects are at 85%

  completion and are expected to be completed by December 31, 2022. Also, the Big Data platform established at the Kenya Agriculture and

  Livestock Research Organization (KALRO) with the support from the project has now a farmer database of 2.35 million farmers and all of these

  farmers are receiving integrated agro weather and market advisories.

  Given the continued good progress since the last implementation support mission across all components both the PDO and IP ratings are being

  retained as "Satisfactory".
