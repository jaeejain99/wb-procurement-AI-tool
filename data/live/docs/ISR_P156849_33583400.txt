 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The Project is making progress to achieve its project development objective (PDO) which is to improve research, teaching, and institutional

  capacity at selected autonomous universities and strengthen the national higher education management system. The four PDO indicators are

  progressing and are expected to be fully achieved by project closing. The project implementation performance is steadily improving and

  accelerating despite the constraints of COVID-19. Twenty three contracts for civil works, goods and consulting services were awarded during last

  year for a total value equivalent to US$75 million. Many important activities related to training, research and accreditation have been

  completed. The project restructuring, however, has been delayed and is expected to be completed only by mid November 2021.
