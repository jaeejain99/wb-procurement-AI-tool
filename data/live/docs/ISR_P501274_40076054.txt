  The Host and Rohingya Enhancement of Lives Project (HELP) is a US$ 350 million project which will finance infrastructure
  and services to increase the resilience of the Host Communities and Displaced Rohingya Population (DRP) through (i)
  Resilient Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) (ii) Climate and Disaster Resilient Infrastructure, Energy and Emergency
  Response and (iii) Strengthening Institutional Systems to Enhance Service Provision and Support Resilient Development.
  This is a multi-ministerial project and being implemented by the following implementing agencies through their
  respective Project Implementation Units (PIUs):
      • Department of Public Health Engineering (DPHE) (Component 1) and Local Government Engineering Department
           (LGED) (Subcomponent 2.1) - Local Government Division, Ministry of Local Government, Rural Development, and
           Cooperatives (MoLGRDC)
      • Roads and Highways Department (RHD) (Subcomponent 2.2) - Road Transport and Highways Division, Ministry of
           Road Transport and Bridges (MoRTB)
      • Bangladesh Power Development Board (BPDB) (Subcomponent 2.3) - Power Division, Ministry of Power, Energy
           and Mineral Resources (MoPEMR).
      • The Bangladesh Forest Department (BFD) and Department of Environment (DoE) under Ministry of Environment,
           Forest and Climate Change are carrying out afforestation and environmental impact assessments, respectively,
           under Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) with LGED.
      • Central Coordination Unit (CCU) led by Economic Relations Division (ERD)

  The Project has achieved remarkable 70% of afforestation target with 2,470 hectares completed and 5 operational
  rainwater harvesting systems, demonstrating strong field-level execution capacity. Procurement of remaining activities
  are ongoing.
