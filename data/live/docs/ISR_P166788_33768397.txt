 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Implementation Review and Support mission of the Nepal DPF with Cat DDO. This mission was conducted by a Bank team on Feb 28-March

  4, 2022, with the main objective to: (i) assess the progress on the results indicators, as part of the monitoring and implementation support activities

  of this operation; and (ii) identify any areas where the Bank may provide technical support. During the reporting period and despite the difficult

  situation due to COVID-19, including a third wave of the outbreak largely due to the Omicron variant, and the historic monsoon precipitation, the

  GON has been able to successfully meet three out of four results indicators, and the fourth one, related to health preparedness and response, is

  on track to be achieved during the project implementation period.
