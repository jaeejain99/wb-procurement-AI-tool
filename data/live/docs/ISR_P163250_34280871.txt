 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Overall project implementation is supervised by a Project Management Unit within Palli Karma-Sahayak Foundation (PKSF). The progress towards

  achieving the Project Development Objective (PDO) “to increase the adoption of environmentally sustainable practices by targeted

  microenterprises” is on track. As of September 2023, Sustainable Enterprise Project had introduced environmentally sustainable practices in 30

  priority sub-sectors/clusters, 25 of which were also provided with common services that target microenterprises (MEs') productivity. Beyond the

  cumulative target of 40,000 MEs by the 5th Year, 65,124 MEs (among which 84% are female) had signed loan agreements with Partner

  Organizations under the project.
