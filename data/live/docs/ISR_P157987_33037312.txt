 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The following is a summary of implementation progress in some of the key activities under each component during the MTR and agreements
  reached:
           Component A. Improving the Coordination and Management of Statistical Activities: Under this component the project has made
            progress in the review of Memorandum of Understanding (MoUs) with 9 agencies, setting up of the National Advisory Council on Statistics
            (NACS), and translation of the Statistics Act (accessible from the BBS website). The PIU has completed translation of the Statistics Act to
            English and made it available on the BBS website. The BBS with support from the TP will continue putting in place institutional framework
            for coordination of the National Statistical System (NSS).

             Component B. Developing Human Resources and ICT Infrastructure to Produce and Manage Data: Key activities delivered under
              component B include: Training Policy that covers all BBS staff, prioritizing participation of women and field-based staff and a draft training
              plan based on the training policy. A training inventory document has also been produced containing training opportunities in statistics
              offered by national and international organizations aimed at building a sustainable statistical capacity through efficient, effective, and
              harmonized delivery of training at the country and regional levels. Given the COVID-19 situation the MTR recommended combining both
              online and face-to-face courses once circumstances allow. The project has also supported ‘Training on Agriculture Statistics’ for a total of
              478 staff of BBS working both at Headquarters and at the Field Offices in the late June 2019, and two separate advance Training on
              Microdata Analysis using STATA. The MTR mission however noted the considerable delay in procurement of the ICT infrastructure due to
              delay in preparation of the ICT Plan. The team encourage the staff of the BBS Computer Wing and the Project IT consultant to work
              together and provide necessary information to the TP experts on time so that they can develop the draft ICT Plan.
             Component C. Improving the Coverage and Quality of Core Statistics Required for Policy Making: Under this component a
              comprehensive booklet on Harmonization of the Definitions of all Statistical Indicators has been produced. In total, more than 2,200
              definitions were standardized with inputs from international experts and BBS staff through six consultative workshops with different BBS
              Wings. Through the project support, BBS was also able to conduct and complete the Rapid Phone Survey on Livelihood and a survey
              report produced and disseminated. The project is further providing support to preparatory work and technical support to the Household
              Income and Expenditure Survey (HIES) 2021-22 and Labor Force Survey (LFS) 2021-22.
             Component D. Promoting and Strengthening Access to and the Use of Official Statistics: The MTR mission observed that this
              component has had challenges in completing some of the key tasks including the Data Licensing Agreement between BBS and World
              Bank, finalization and implementation of an effective and clear dissemination policy, and the preparation of Advance Release Calendar
              and the Protocol of Advance Release Calendar. Given the importance of these activities for attaining the Project Development Objectives,
              it was agreed that the PMU will prioritize the discussion on the Data Licensing Agreement. The Twinning Partner with support and input
              from BBS will prepare the Advance Release Calendar and Protocol of Advance Release Calendar, and the dissemination policy.

 Overall Comments PDO

  The MTR clarified and agreed on the number of core statistics and specific surveys or data to be improved under each domain for the
  purposes of tracking the achievement of PDO level indicators. The findings from the MTR mission have therefore led to proposed clarification
  of what constitute core statistics and surveys in relation to the PDO level indicators, and how improvement will be measured. The MTR also
  proposed revision of target for PDO indicator 2 “Core statistics are released according to a pre-defined calendar” by half from 80% to 40% to
  make it more realistic. This request will be made at the project restructuring request stage.

  While the project document clearly stipulates that two rounds of User Satisfaction Survey (USS) will be carried out to measure the progress
  over time by an external independent agency for indicator 4 "Increased User Satisfaction Survey", no such survey has been conducted. The
  MTR has recommended that in the interest of time, the PIU will carry out an initial USS while an independent agency will be contracted to
  undertake two additional follow-ups of the survey – one towards the end of 2021 and the latter towards the end of the project to assess
  progress over time.
