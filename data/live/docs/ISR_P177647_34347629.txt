 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Program Status. The School Sector Transformation Program (SSTP) Operation was approved on March 24, 2023 by the World Bank's Board of
  Executive Directors'. The Operation includes financing from International Development Association for an amount of US$120 million, and financing
  from Global Partnership for Education for an amount of US$19.7 million. The signing of the Financing Agreement and the Grant Agreement was
  completed on May 31, 2023. The SSTP, together with other Joint Financing Partners (JFPs) and non-JFP development partners supports the
  implementation of the School Education Sector Plan (SESP) 2022-3023. Overall, the program is making good progress towards achieving the
  program development objectives to improve literacy and numeracy as measured through the national assessment of reading and numeracy (NARN)
  and to increase the number of local-level governments that receive performance grants.
  Key Decisions. The recent Budget Review Meeting (BRM) for the SESP stressed the importance of its swift enactment of the Education Bill to
  strengthen and institutionalize the coordination and collaboration across the different tiers of government in the school education sector. Capacity
  building of local-level governments and schools is critical to mitigate the institutional risks, accelerate the project implementation and improve the
  impacts on students at the school level.
  Citizen Engagement. The government and JFPs invited two key representatives of the civil society supporting school education, the Association of
  International Non-Governmental Organizations in Nepal (AIN) and the National Campaign for Education for Nepal (NCE-Nepal) to the BRM. Their
  participation substantially contributed to understanding of on-going collaboration as well as potential further collaboration between the federal and
  local governments and civil society in enhancing the access and quality of school education at the local level, including awareness raising among
  local education stakeholders about education rights and rights of marginalized and school enrolment campaign. BRM joint field trips by the
  government and Development Partners gave an excellent opportunity to observe the SESP implementation on the ground and hear feedback from
  schools, communities and parents for the improvement of SESP to better serve the local stakeholders.
