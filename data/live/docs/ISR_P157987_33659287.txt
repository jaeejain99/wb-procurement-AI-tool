 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Mechanisms to enhance coordination: The National Advisory Council on Statistics (NACS) that was set up through the project and approved by
  Honorable Prime Minister held its first meeting on June 27, 2021. The NACS is to meet at least once a year to provide overall guidance to the
  National Statistical System (NSS). The vision of the NSS of Bangladesh is to become a world-class provider of authentic, relevant, and timely data
  in a holistic and user-friendly manner. The NACS Committee is comprised of high-level members from different government agencies producing
  statistics and coordinated by BBS.
  Investments in ICT infrastructure to support data collection and management: The project has procured several ICT equipment, including
  computers, printers, and other equipment’s for office space. Given the delay in preparation of the ICT Plan all the currently procured ICT equipment
  are based on the immediate need and as per requirements of the various wings of BBS. The Twinning Partner experts, Project IT consultant and

  the staff of BBS Computer Wing are working on developing the draft ICT Plan. Several consultative meetings between the TP and BBS Computer
  Wing were held in order to make an efficient and effective ICT Plan that will cover the entire statistical process i.e., from data collection to data
  dissemination with the goal of building an integrated system for the production and dissemination of statistics.
  Implementation of training policy to develop human resources: The TP has prepared a draft Training Policy in line with the international best
  practices to cover all BBS staff, prioritizing participation of women and field-based staff (Division, Zila (district) and Upazila (sub-district) level).
  Based on Training Policy, the TP has also developed a Training Plan and will finalize soon training Inventory document. So far, the team has
  conducted several key basic and intermediate trainings, including:
        a. Training on Agriculture Statistics: The first and second rounds of the ‘Training on Agriculture Statistics’ for a total of 1,500 staff of BBS
             working both at Headquarters and at the Field Offices have been conducted. The training follows a Pretest and a Post-test evaluation.
        b. Advance Training on Microdata Analysis using STATA and R: BBS through the TP has conducted 3 batches of STATA training. In
             total 60 participants were trained. The first Batch of the training was held at BBS HQ from 8-19 November 2020, with a total number of 24
             officials from BBS HQ and Field office participating. The second batch was held between February 28 – March 11, 2021 and the third
             between March 21 – April 1, 2021. BBS also organized training course on “Data Management and Statistical Analysis Using R” for 20
             participants between September 5-14, 2021. The objective of this course was to increase the output and analysis of data within the BBS
             by using R language. Other additional trainings conducted include a 10-day training session for 35 participants at Bogura RDA on
             Sampling Techniques and Practice from October 4 – 14, 2021[1]: and 2 days Training for 13 participants on Price Statistics organized at
             BBS HQ from October 23 -24, 2021.
  Assessments and training for better management systems: The TP has prepared and completed training material on quality management, HR
  Recruitment, and a manual for HR management in BBS. The report on current practices in HR Recruitment and Manual for HR Management in BBS
  aims at providing guidelines for improving the recruitment process of staff at the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS), specifically, survey
  enumerators. The focus on this group of employees had been decided during the inception mission of the Key Expert on national statistical
  coordination (January 2021), in view of the important operations that the BBS is planning (Population Census, Household Income and Expenditure
  Survey, etc.).
  Improvement of Core statistics and Surveys: The TP has conducted baseline review for several core statistics. These include baseline reviews
  for demography and vital statistics, population and housing census, health statistics, gender statistics, labor and industry statistics, price statistics,
  national account statistics, crop statistics, land use statistics and livestock statistics have been completed. However, BBS has requested that
  Strength Weaknesses Opportunities and Threats (SWOT) and Gap analysis are conducted to finalize these reviews. It has been agreed with BBS
  that the SWOT and Gap analysis will be completed as soon as the key experts conduct their planned missions to Bangladesh. So far missions of
  the key experts responsible for the Price Statistics, National Accounts and Agriculture statistics have been conducted, and SWOT and Gap analysis
  for these statistical domains developed.
  Website and microdata anonymization: To support the key project goal of microdata anonymization and making data accessible, BBS has shared
  core surveys including on Health Statistics (MICS-2019, HMS-2014), Labor Statistics (LFS 2016-17), Industry Statistics (SMI-2019) and
  Poverty/Welfare Statistics (HIES-2016). The metadata documentation for the core statistical fields of BBS has been produced. It adheres to
  internationally accepted formats for metadata for the construction of metadata. As specified in the Mid-Tern Review report, metadata will be
  produced for the Health and Mortality Survey, Labour Force Survey, Survey of Manufacturing Industries, Consumer Price Index, Quarterly National
  Accounts, Crop Statistics, Fisheries statistics and the Household Income and Expenditure Survey.
  Support to 2022 HIES and LFS: After prolonged delays both HIES and the LFS surveys are now under process for implementation by January
  2022. While HIES will be implemented between January – December, 2022, LFS will be a quarterly survey to be implemented every 3 months from
  January 2022 to December 2023.
  Twinning Partnership (TP). The progress in implementation of the TP contract is satisfactory having successfully completed almost a half of the
  deliverables. The TP has shared the 6th quarterly report which describes the activities of the Twinning partnership carried out during the period of 8
  July 2021 – 7 October 2021. The report is currently under review.
  Workshop on “Moving Bangladesh Towards a World-Class Statistical Producer”: Twining Partner (TP) together with the PIU organized a half-
  day workshop hosted at the Pan Pacific Sonargaon hotel on September 23rd, 2021. The objective of the workshop was to bring together
  participants from different Wings of Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS), Project Implementation Unit (PIU), and the Twinning Partner to discuss
  and review the status, progress, and challenges of project implementation of key project activities. The Workshop was also aimed to facilitate the
  collaboration between counterparts in the project team, BBS Wings and the PIU, and was also an opportunity for the team to present all capacity
  building activities undertaken by the TP and foster networking within the statistical community in Bangladesh. The focus was to support
  improvement in BBS staff capabilities to produce and disseminate consistent and timely quality statistics responding to the needs of the various
  users; mainstreaming sectors and other players into the National Statistics System (NSS); coordinating the entire NSS; responding to data
  challenges; delivering a country-led data revolution; and building statistical capacity across the “the statistical value chain”.

 Overall Comments PDO

  While it has been challenging to obtain microdata for anonymization and creating metadata, BBS has shared key datasets with the TP expert
  who is now processing the data. Baseline review of core socioeconomic statistics have also been completed. There has been considerable
  delay in the implementation of the User Satisfaction Survey (USS). BBS is planning to undertake the USS by December 2021.
