4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Project implementation for the Jamuna River Sustainable Management Project 1 has commenced, with all three Project
  Directors fully engaged, along with assigned staff from their respective agencies. The Bangladesh Water Development
  Board has updated the technical design for the river bank protection structures for Fulchari in Gainbandha District and
  Kalihati in Tangail District. Before the onset of the 2024 monsoon season BWDB strengthened the embankment at
  Kalihati to stop the rampant river bank erosion, which has helped to stabilize the river embankment.
