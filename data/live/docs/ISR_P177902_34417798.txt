4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  This ISR is based on the fourth implementation review and support of the Nepal: ACCESS, conducted from March 25-April
  17, 2024.
  The Project is making some notable progress for the Transport Component (2A and 2B) such as, procurement process of
  Butwal-Gurusinghe (BG) road works is now taking pace; critical actions to facilitate implementation is completed with
  fully staffing project site offices; and 50 percent of the project sites received clearance for contractors to start the works
  before mobilization. On the other hand, activities under the other two key components i.e., Digital System for Trade (1)
  and Green Resilient corridor (2C) activities, are progressing slow. Some of the critical delayed actions under the Trade
  Component include, pending decision by the government on the institutional status of Nepal Intermodal Transport
  Development Board (NITDB); and the changes in the Director General (DG) positions for both the Department of Custom
  (DoC) and the Department of Commerce, Supplies and Consumer Protection (DoCSCP). On the Green Resilient corridor,
  inadequate counterpart funding has prevented the Department of Forests and Soil Conservation (DoFSC) from executing
  the planned activities. Given the overall slow implementation and negligible disbursement (about US$0.42 million) since
  effectiveness over a year, the Implementation Progress is downgraded to “Moderately Unsatisfactory”. The team
  informed the government that once contracts are awarded for the Butwal-Gurusinge road works (2A) and the
  implementation progress of other components under MoICS and MoFE are accelerated, thus meeting disbursement
  targets, the rating for Implementation Progress will be revisited in the next review. Considering the progress on key
  activities, performance, and identified actions to advance implementation, the likelihood of achieving the PDO is retained
  as “Satisfactory”.

4.3 Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Status (Public Disclosure)
  The ACCESS MPA Program was approved by the Board on June 28, 2022. The Nepal and Bangladesh Phase 1 Projects
  became effective on February 10, 2023, and May 3, 2023, respectively. While project implementation has started to pick
  up pace, both projects lag the original schedule due to the extensive time delay between World Bank Board approval and
  project effectiveness. The Royal Government of Bhutan (RGoB) has requested financing for the ACCESS Bhutan Phase 2
  project, which is expected to be approved in Fiscal Year 2025. The ACCESS program regional coordination and peer-to-
  peer learning platform has been launched with several stakeholder coordination and knowledge sharing events (physical
  and virtual) organized.
