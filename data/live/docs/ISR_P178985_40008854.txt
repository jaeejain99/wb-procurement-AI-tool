1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 02-Jul-2024)
   The project development objectives (PDO) are to: (i) enhance access to climate resilient urban infrastructure and services
   in selected clusters, and (ii) strengthen urban management capacity in selected urban centers.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  World Bank financing for the project was declared effective on January 14, 2025. The Project Director was appointed on
  March 21, 2025, and the majority of the staff of the Project Management Unit are on board. Goods packages for
  approximately US$1 million were tendered on April 6, 2025. The contract of the Design Supervision and Monitoring
  Consultants is expected to be signed by the end of April 2025.
