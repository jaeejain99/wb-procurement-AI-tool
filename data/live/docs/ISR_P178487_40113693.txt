3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The IDA Board of Executive Directors approved the Program on September 22, 2023. On October 31, 2023, the Executive
Committee of National Economic Council (ECNEC), approved the LAISE Program. The legal documents were signed by the
Country Director of the World Bank and the Senior Secretary of the Economic Relations Division of the Government on
November 22, 2023. The Bank has declared Program effectiveness on November 30, 2023. The project is on track to
achieve seven out of the eight Year 2 Disbursement Linked Results.
The total disbursement remains US$142.9 million, representing 46.8% of the total credit, underscoring the effective pace
of implementation and results delivery. Directorate of Secondary and Higher Education has submitted the semi annual
monitoring report for period covering January to June 2026 and environmental and social safeguard report for quarter
ending March 2026
