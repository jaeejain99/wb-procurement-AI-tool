 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Good progress has been made in implementing project activities since the project's restructuring and the last mission of the Bank in December

  2022, motivating a continuation of the overall progress rating of Satisfactory (S).

  Component 2 of the project (developing tourism sites and destinations), however, continues to trail the progress of other project components, in part

  due to the delayed start of select contracts, such as the contract for the design of Cape Coast Castle and the Protected Areas (Kakum, Mole, Shai

  Hills). The project has made satisfactory progress towards achieving the project development objective (PDO), as all but one PDO-level indicators

  have now been met. The last PDO level indicator (length of stay of domestic tourists at select tourist sites) is expected to be met in July 2023.

  Component 1: Strengthening the tourism enabling environment: there have been several positive developments. These include the further roll-out

  of capacity building training to key industry stakeholders, ongoing international and domestic marketing campaigns, and ongoing training of trainers

  of Lower-Level Tourism and Hospitality skills. Training on the tourism sector's regulations targeted 150 private sector providers and other

  workshops and training events for industry players including tour guides, taxi drivers, artists etc. was completed.

  Component 2: Developing tourism sites and destinations. Several tourist sites and destinations have now received infrastructural upgrades and

  developments, including the Kintampo Waterfalls, Bonwire Crafts Village, Bogyawe Palace Museum—Akwamufie, Ankasa Forest Reserve

  Receptive Facility, Nzulezu Receptive Facility and Gushegu Smock Village. Notably, the development of the Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park was

  almost completed by the time of the mission. The renovation work at KNMP is now complete, with the official opening being July 4, 2023.

  Component 3: Tourism enterprise support program: The second round of grants under MOTAC's administration was successfully implemented

  within the projected timeframe; as such, the US$10m reallocated to the grants program in June 2022 was disbursed by early 2023 for a total

  of 1,249 grants, representing 3 grant windows (COVID recovery grants (1002), SME grants (206) and site upgrade grants (41)); out of which 55% of

  the grant beneficiaries were females.
