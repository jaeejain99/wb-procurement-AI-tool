 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Following restructuring on December 22, 2024, project implementation came to an end on December 31, 2024. Despite substantial delays during

  the initial years of implementation (including a long lag from approval to effectiveness), the project virtually achieved its revised coastal forest

  planting/rehabilitation targets. While Component 3 (Generating Sustainable Benefits from Coastal Forests) was significantly scaled down through

  the restructuring, the project was able to deliver substantial livelihood benefits to the local communities through the subprojects undertaken under

  this component. Strong community engagement has been observed at the project sites, including by local villagers contracted to undertake planting

  and tending after receiving technical training. About 40-50 villagers per commune are involved in sandy soil and mangrove planting and

  rehabilitation at each visited site. There is also evidence that farmers in the project area have acquired new skills and increased income from non-

  timber forest products as a result of better forest management. Communities are also beginning to benefit from improved forest protection

  infrastructure such as roads that improve the communities’ mobility and their access to markets, livelihood opportunities, and social services. There
