 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The Ghana Productive Safety Net Project (GPSNP) faced programming delays between April and June 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic and

  the partial lockdown that occurred as a result. Even in the midst of the initial challenges, the project was able to proceed with operational activities

  after some adjustments made to protect the health and safety of beneficiaries and program implementers. Continued implementation of GPSNP

  was crucial in supporting the poor and vulnerable during the challenging times.

  The following results have been achieved over the period: (i) Labor-Intensive Public Works (LIPW) provided short-term employment to 34,246

  beneficiaries, surpassing their target of 30,000; (ii) the productive inclusion program has enrolled 8,669 beneficiaries for the first phase of the

  intervention. These beneficiaries completed trainings, and the disbursement of cash grants begun in April 2021 and will be completed in June 2021,

  after which beneficiaries will initiate their new small enterprises; (iii) In addition to being instrumental in providing data for COVID-19 pandemic relief

  activities, the Ghana National Household Registry (GNHR) was able to complete data collection in the three Northern regions by December 2020,

  reaching almost 400,000 households, data dissemination activities are underway; (iv) Over 100,000 LEAP cash transfers beneficiaries were

  enrolled in the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) with the goal of reaching 100 percent coverage of LEAP beneficiaries by the end of 2021;

  (v) The Helpline of Hope Call Center of the Single Window Citizens Engagement Service (SWCES) continued to play an essential role in providing

  information and receiving grievances, particularly in support of the temporary cash transfers that were delivered as part of COVID-19 relief

  efforts. A total of 6,322 calls were received during the period under review (with over 2000 prank calls). From these calls, 2,209 cases were

  opened, and 1,974 cases were resolved.

  In August 2020, GPSNP underwent Level-2 Restructuring to reallocate funds towards cash transfers to support economic relief of LEAP cash

  transfers beneficiaries, but also to reach 125,000 additional poor and vulnerable individuals not on safety net programming. Existing social

  protection systems were used for this activity. By April 2021, all targeted beneficiaries had received their first tranche, with 60,000 remaining to

  receive their final tranche by May 31, 2021.

  The program faced challenges in providing timely disbursements of cash transfers to LEAP beneficiaries. Continued advocacy is underway to

  ensure that administrative transitions do not cause delays in cash disbursements.

  The design of the Ghana Productive Safety Net 2 (GPSNP 2), the US$ 100 million follow on social protection project, was completed and was

  approved by the World Bank Executive Board on March 30, 2021. The project is currently in the process of receiving national approvals. The design

  of this project will ensure that there will be no gap in social protection programming, and more beneficiaries can be supported, particularly in urban

  areas.
