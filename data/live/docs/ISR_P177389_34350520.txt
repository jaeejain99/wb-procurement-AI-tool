 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Nepal Quality Health Systems Program-for-Results operation became effective on January 10, 2024. The Program includes financing of
  US$100 million from the International Development Association (IDA), and US$3.84 million from the Health Emergency Preparedness and
  Response (HEPR) Trust Fund to strengthen Nepal's efforts to deliver quality healthcare services, ensure financial protection to its citizens,
  especially to the poor and vulnerable, and build resilient public health systems.
  Citizen Engagement. The Program will facilitate implementation of minimum service standards at the selected Province and local level hospitals in
  Koshi and Gandaki Provinces, along with social audits, which will enhance citizen engagement and accountability. Social audit feedback will guide
  the health facility quality improvement program. The Program will conduct extensive community outreach to enroll and retain poor population in the
  health insurance scheme, ensuring access to healthcare and financial protection. Citizens are expected to actively participate in disease
  surveillance, emergency preparedness, and response plans at local levels.
