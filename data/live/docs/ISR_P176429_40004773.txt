1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 19-Dec-2024)
   The Project Development Objectives (PDOs) are to (i) increase access to safely managed water supply in Chattogram; and
   (ii) improve operational performance and financial sustainability of Chattogram Water Supply and Sewerage Authority
   (CWASA).
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project was approved by the World Bank’s Board on December 19, 2024. The Financial Agreement (FA) shall be
  signed after the Government of Bangladesh (GOB) processes the approval of the Development Project Proposal (DPP) for
  the project. The process of assigning staff to the PMU is now under way, and the recruitment of individual support
  consultants is also in progress. The appointment of the Project Director for the PMU is awaited. The DPP for this project is
  awaiting the approval by the Executive Committee of National Economic Council (ECNEC) of the GOB. The project
  implementing entity i.e. Chattogram Water Supply and Sewerage Authority (CWASA) is required to establish the full
  project management arrangements at CWASA, and implement the key project start-up activities especially the key
  technical assistance activities that will govern the investment activities during the project implementation period.



4.2 MPA Program Development Objective
  Original MPA Program Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval (Seq. 1) package on 19-Dec-2024)
  To (i) improve access to safely-managed water supply and sanitation services, (ii) reduce pollution in rivers and canals,
  and (iii) enhance resilience to flood risks in selected urban areas.

  Current MPA Program Development Objective (Approved as part of (Seq. ) package on )




4.3 Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Status (Public Disclosure)
  The preparations for the second phase of the MPA namely Metro Dhaka Water Security and Resilience Program
  (P506770) is underway following the Project Concept review held on December 4, 2024.
