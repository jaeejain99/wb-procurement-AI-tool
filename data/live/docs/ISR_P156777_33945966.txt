 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  KUSP implementation progress is on track to achieve the project development objective. Apart from the 59 beneficiary municipalities, KUSP has

  catalyzed the formation of an additional 17 municipalities. Of the additional 17, 7 (Elwak, Mwingi, Lietein, Diani, Kitengela, Bondo and Ugunja) have

  a municipal charter gazetted, and urban boards and municipal managers appointed. The remaining 10 have only municipal charters gazetted. This

  demonstrates that the program had institutional development impacts beyond the targeted 59 established municipalities. In terms of urban

  infrastructure and services, KUSP has supported 329 investments across the 59 municipalities. These investments are on connectivity, socio-

  economy, storm water drainage, waste management, and fire and disaster management. Of the 329 investments, about 68% (223) are complete

  with the remainder under different stages of implementation. Of the 106 ongoing projects, 51 are above 65% completion.
