 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The project completed the construction of two pre-schools in Huong Son and Phan Dinh in June 2021 and both have been put into operation and

  have achieved their intended results with respect to improved student to teacher ratios. The construction of Dan bridge is nearing completion and is

  expected to be turned over for operation in the 1st quarter of calendar year 2022. Following delays in the first two years of project implementation,

  overall project implementation has improved in the past seven months. All project-financed activities are on track to be completed and the project is

  on-track to achieve the targeted project development objectives by the project closing date of December 31, 2023.
