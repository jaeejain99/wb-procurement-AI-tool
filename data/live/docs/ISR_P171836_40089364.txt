4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Project implementation has accelerated after a slow start. Six upgradation contracts (69.93 km, US$13.92 Million) have
  been awarded across Madhesh, Sudurpaschim, and Karnali, and works have commenced. Detailed Project Reports
  (DPRs) for an additional 307 km are under preparation and finalization across the three provinces. For new
  connectivity, DPRs of roads (51 kms) for two unconnected municipalities—Sigas and Dolpa—are nearing completion,
  while a consultant is being hired for Saipal. Periodic maintenance covering 255 km is progressing, with procurement
  advancing across provinces. Routine maintenance work is initiated in Madhesh, with community-based road
  maintenance groups formed and work underway. The immediate priority is to accelerate DPR finalization and
  tendering to sustain momentum.
