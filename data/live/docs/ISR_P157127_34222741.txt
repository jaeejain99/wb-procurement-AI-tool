 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Overall Progress. Although substantial delays during the initial years of implementation (including a long lag from approval to effectiveness) will

  likely require adjustment of some project activities and output targets, current implementation performance is promising. The provinces have made

  significant progress in coastal forest planting and rehabilitation with the cumulative completion rate (area completed/proposed revised target) having

  reached 86.8 percent by October 10, 2023. Only Quang Ninh province is currently conducting additional planting/rehabilitation to cover small

  remaining areas. It is anticipated that by project closing the project will have completed planting in most of the planned planting/rehabilitation areas

  except for areas excluded due to unforeseen circumstances and technical issues affecting survival of seedings. While substantial progress has

  been made under Component 3 (Generating Sustainable Benefits from Coastal Forests), there remains outstanding work to be completed. Strong

  community engagement has been observed at the project sites, including by local villagers contracted to undertake planting and tending after

  receiving technical training. About 40-50 villagers per commune are involved in sandy soil and mangrove planting and rehabilitation at each visited

  site. There is also evidence that farmers in the project area have acquired new skills and increased income from non-timber forest products as a
