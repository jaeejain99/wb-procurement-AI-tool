1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 22-Sep-2023)
   The Program Development Objective (PDO) is to improve student learning and retention, and build system resilience in
   secondary education.

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The IDA Board of Executive Directors approved the Program on September 22, 2023. On October 31, 2023, the Executive
   Committee of National Economic Council (ECNEC), approved the LAISE Program. The legal documents were signed by the
   World Bank and Economic Relations Division(ERD) of the Government on November 22, 2023. The Bank has declared
   Program effectiveness on November 30, 2023
   The project has successfully submitted all documentation related to the achievement of Year 1 DLRs, including detailed
   records of the achievement process, to IMED through SHED, MoE— a key milestone demonstrating strong delivery
   momentum
   The total disbursement remains US$70.9 million, representing 24% of the total credit, underscoring the effective pace of
   implementation and results delivery
   Directorate of Secondary and Higher Education (DSHE) has submitted the Interim Unaudited Financial Report (IUFR) for the
   quarter ending December 31, 2024, and is currently preparing subsequent reports, further strengthening financial reporting
   and accountability mechanisms
