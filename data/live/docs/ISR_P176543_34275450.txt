 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To expand access to broadband in Project areas, to improve the capacity of individuals to engage in the digital economy, and to enhance the
  foundations for digital government.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overall progress is rated Unsatisfactory as there has been no progress in implementation since approval (in June 2022) and effectiveness (March
  2023), and there is a risk of not achieving the PDO per original plans. Implementation Progress (IP) is rated Unsatisfactory due to various project
  management-related actions and consultancies not being completed as scheduled.
