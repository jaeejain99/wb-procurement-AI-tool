4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
Civil works contracts and key institutional support consultancies are expected to be awarded by May 2027, to support improved access to water supply and improved service sustainability. Similarly, procurement progress is on-going to award contract for piped water supply and on-site sanitation works in Low Income Communities (LIC). Timely award of the major water supply and DMA Formation Lots 1 and 2 contracts is critical, as any further delays could jeopardize completion of construction and successful commissioning within the 42-month period before the project closing date of December 31, 2030.
