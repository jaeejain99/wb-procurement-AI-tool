 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Overall Progress. Overall, the provinces have made significant progress in coastal forest planting and rehabilitation with the cumulative completion

  rate (area completed/revised target) having surpassed 80% by November 30, 2022. As planting/rehabilitation activities are ongoing in central

  provinces, the completion rate is expected to reach around 85% at the end of the 2022 planting season (which lasts until March 2023 for some

  provinces). On the other hand, progress in non-planting activities (small-scale infra, technology, and livelihood subprojects) vary across the

  provinces – the main reason for the low level of disbursement to date (approximately US$30 million).

  Strong community engagement was observed in the project sites visited by the implementation support mission in December 2022. Farmers were

  contracted to undertake planting and tending after receiving technical training. About 40-50 farmers per commune were involved in sandy soil

  planting and mangrove restoration in each visited site. The interviewed farmers were satisfied with their increased income, the new skills they have

  acquired as well as income from non-timber forest products (NTFPs) owing to better managed forests. Communities also benefit from improved
