 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The project completed the construction of two kindergartens in Huong Son and Phan Dinh Phung in August 2021 and both have been put into

  operation and have achieved their intended results with respect to improved student to teacher ratios. The construction of Dan bridge was

  completed and put into service in June 2022 while the upgrading of Dong Bam residential area road was completed in April 2023. The envisaged

  targets for user satisfaction with the new or improved roads and public spaces under the project have been exceeded. All project-financed activities

  are on track to be completed and the project is expected to achieve the targeted project development objectives by the project closing date of

  December 31, 2023.
