4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  On 28 May 2024, the Executive Directors of the International Development Association (IDA) approved US$350 million to
  provide basic human capital services for both the host communities and displaced Rohingya population in Bangladesh.
  The US$350 million Inclusive Services and Opportunities for Host Community and Displaced Rohingya Population (DRP)
  Project (ISOP) will provide support to the Bangladeshi host communities and the DRP through: (i) Building Community
  Resilience and Self Reliance, (ii) Supporting Education Opportunities for Children, (iii) Enhancing Social Care and Economic
  Empowerment, and (iv) Healthier Communities. The project has six implementing agencies, as follows: Ministry of
  Women and Children Affairs, Directorate of Primary Education, Department of Social Services, Directorate General of
  Health Services, Department of Disaster Management, and Directorate General of Family Planning. ISOP was declared
  effective on 19 June 2024.
  Following political events in July 2024, required Government Orders to allow the establishment of implementation
  arrangements were delayed. These approvals were provided by Government of Bangladesh on September 24, 2024. As of
  March 2025, all Project Directors have been designated and PIUs are being steadily staffed. The Project Operations
  Manuals are also being finalized. Key procurements and contracts, especially of United Nations agencies, are expected to
  be completed by 30 April 2025, allowing health, education, and social protection services to reach the intended
  vulnerable DRP and host communities.
