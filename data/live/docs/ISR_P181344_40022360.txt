1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 08-Oct-2024)
   The development objective is to strengthen the capacity of the Government of Nepal to manage disaster and climate
   risks, including disease outbreaks.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
        •       The National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority (NDRRMA) confirmed the transfer of
                Disaster Management Funds (DMF) to local governments from the Central Disaster Management Fund
                (RI#1).

        •       The Ministry of Urban Development - Department of Urban Development and Building Construction
                (DUDBC) has initiated procurement process for a Centralized - Electronic Building Permit System (eBPS)
                and began prioritizing Municipalities for integration (RI#2, IRI#2.1).

       •       The Department of Hydrology and Meteorology (DHM) in collaboration with the Nepal Telecom and NCELL
               issued early warnings during the September 2024 disasters and has prioritized five hazards (including,
               floods, heavy rain, drought, heatwaves, and coldwaves) for future alerts (RI#3, IRI#3.1).

       •       The Department of Water Resources and Irrigation (DoWRI), is developing River Management Plans (RMPs)
               for the Balan and Daraudi rivers, targeted for completion by June 2025 (RI#4).

       •       The Ministry of Health and Population (MoHP) through its Early Warning and Response System (EWARS) is
               monitoring 15 diseases, syndromes and conditions, up from a baseline of 6 in 2024 and assessing the
               inclusion of additional diseases (RI#5).
