 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The project remains on track to achieving its objectives and is expected to complete all activities three months ahead of the current December 31,

  2022 closing date. The project has significantly improved its implementation pace and marked the following achievements: (a) 51 Percent of

  beneficiaries have initiated or expanded a household enterprise; (b) average annual earnings of unskilled workers in LIPWs has significantly

  increased, exceeding the project target; (c) 70 percent of respondents reporting that the assets (sub-projects) created through LIPW improved their

  livelihoods; (d) steadily increasing the percent of beneficiaries who are identified as extremely poor according to targeting criteria (56 percent); and

  (e) ensuring that LIPW payments and LEAP grants are made on time to beneficiaries (56 percent) while supporting 100 percent of beneficiaries

  open a bank account.

                                       LEAP

                                       LEAP operates with bi-monthly payment schedule with predetermined payment dates indicated on the

                                       LEAP calendar. All payments made on the stated dates on the calendar are considered payments made

                                       on time. Thus, the percentage is calculated as number of payments made on the stipulated dates divided

   Comments:

                                       to be made but three have so far been made and one (1) made on time. Percentage payment made on

                                       time therefore is (4+1)/ (6+4) *100= 50%
