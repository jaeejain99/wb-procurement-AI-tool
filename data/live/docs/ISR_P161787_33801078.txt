 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  The Project development objective is to promote financial sector soundness and access to financial services by individuals. The legal agreements

  were signed on December 12, 2018 and the project became effective on June 20, 2019. The pace of implementation has been steadily accelerating

  over the last six months, following a slowdown as a result of COVID-19 lockdowns and travel restrictions.
