 Implementation Status and Key Decisions



  Progress towards the achievement of the Project Development Objectives (PDO) and overall implementation progress are both satisfactory

  The project is progressing well with respect to achievement of all six Project Development Objective (PDO) indicators - (i) reduction of procurement

  time using electronic government procurement (e-GP) has already exceeded the project end target – it takes on an average of 57 days to complete

  procurement against the target of 70 days; (ii) newly selected 916 public sector organizations invited 70% of their procurement through e-GP

  against the project end target of 100%; (iii) citizen monitoring in contract implementation is ongoing in 48 upazilas (sub-districts) against the target of

  45; (iv) development of Implementation Monitoring and Evaluation Division’s (IMED) project management information system (PMIS) for online

  implementation monitoring of projects is moving at full swing with a target to complete the testing phase by April 2022; (v) electronic contract

  management and payment (e-CMS) module of the e-GP has been successfully piloted in three large organizations. It will be rolled-out country-wide

  The project has recently launched an online training portal for the introductory procurement training which can be accessed by anyone free of cost.

  The project has also introduced a Citizen Portal (https://citizen.cptu.gov.bd/) providing public access to procurement and contract implementation
