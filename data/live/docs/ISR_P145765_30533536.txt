 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The recently completed mid-term review confirmed that the project is now on track to meet its objectives. The GCIC has been successfully

  established and is effectively carrying out its core mission to incubate and grow Ghanaian climate technology businesses. It has begun to expand its

  range of activities that impact on the growth of the Ghanaian market for climate technologies. The delay in component 2 implementation has been

  felt in that viable, growing climate technology businesses from the GCIC and otherwise are facing challenges in raising early stage capital.

  Overcoming these implementation delays for component 2 is now in sight and the GCVF should begin contributing to the impact of the project.



  Stakeholders in Ghana’s entrepreneurship ecosystem, government and private sector recognize the value of the GCIC project. Their buy-in is best

  demonstrated by the number of mutually-beneficial partnerships that have been brokered by the GCIC so far, such as a recent collaboration with

  Ghana’s leading multimedia agency, Joy News, that will feature the work of GCIC entrepreneurs in a series of five TV documentaries, showcasing

  their efforts to mitigate the effects of climate change. Earlier this year, the GCIC participated in UK-Ghana Week as part of the business sector and

  trade delegation accompanying H.E. President Akufo-Addo of Ghana to the Commonwealth Heads of Government meetings, in London. In sum, the

  GCIC has become a go-to organization for matters related to green entrepreneurship in Ghana.



  There has also been significant recognition from outside of Ghana. The GCIC’s flagship event, Incubating Innovation, was held in May 2018 and

  attracted global players to discuss the challenges in Ghana’s entrepreneurship ecosystem including seed financing, business management support,

  technology development, and policy to grow Ghana’s green business markets. Participants from countries such as Israel and South Africa offered

  lessons from their own countries’ experiences applicable to building Ghana’s green entrepreneurship ecosystem.



  At mid-term, results are generally tracking to meet targets in the project results framework. The number of businesses and entrepreneurs served is

  slightly below target as the GCIC programs were delayed at the outset of effectiveness. However, the GCIC is expanding the number of businesses

  served and the GCVF is coming online so the target should still be met by project closure. Other indicators, including number of climate friendly

  products delivered to households, companies accessing financing, and revenues generated are at or above intermediate targets. Results reflect

  June 30, 2018 numbers as the monitoring is done quarterly and Sep. 30, 2018 numbers are still being collected.



  Due to the strong performance of the GCIC and implementation of the GCVF getting underway, the project has a strong likelihood of achieving all

  results indicators in the results framework (RF). The Bank team recommended to the implementation teams to raise their ambitions beyond the

  indicators in the results framework so that project targets can be significantly exceeded.
