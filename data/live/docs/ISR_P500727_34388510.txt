 4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


  4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
On May 28, 2024, the Executive Directors of the International Development Association (IDA) approved $350 million to provide basic human capital
services for both the host communities and displaced Rohingya population in Bangladesh. The $350 million Inclusive Services and Opportunities for
Host Community and Displaced Rohingya Population Project (ISOP) will provide support to the Bangladeshi host communities and the Rohingya people
through (i) Building Community Resilience and Self Reliance (ii) Supporting Education Opportunities for Children (iii) Enhancing Social Care and
Economic Empowerment and (iv) Healthier Communities.

The project has six implementing agencies, as follows: Ministry of Women and Children Affairs, Directorate of Primary Education, Department of Social
Services, Directorate General of Health Services, Department of Disaster Management and Directorate General of Family Planning.

ISOP was declared effective on June 19, 2024.
